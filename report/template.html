小鼠眼睛关键点追踪：做了什么，结果是什么Mouse eye keypoint tracking: what was done and what came out

Zhiheng Li (University of Minnesota), Kaiwen Sheng (Stanford) · 页面生成时间page built {{BUILT}} · GitHub: ZhihengLi0/mouse-behavior-tracking

任务：用 DeepLabCut 在小鼠眼睛视频的每一帧上找 8 个点：瞳孔上、下、左、右，上眼睑、下眼睑，鼻侧眼角、颞侧眼角。由这 8 个点得到瞳孔大小、眼睛睁开程度和眨眼，之后与脑记录对齐。

核心问题：拿到一个新视频，人要标多少帧，模型才够用？

工作分成两个阶段，以 2026 年 9 月 20 日明确点位定义为界。第一阶段用的是之前的点位定义，只保留三项不依赖点位定义的选择：batch size、模型、纠错算法。其余结论都来自第二阶段，从同一个 5 分钟视频按明确后的定义重新开始。两个阶段的误差数字不能直接比较。

第一阶段的结论（第 1–3 步）

前提：瞳孔上点标在看得见的、没有被眼睑挡住的瞳孔的最高处。

  1. batch size 用多少？用 2。batch 2、4、8、16 分不出高下，按 20 帧验证帧上的验证损失最低取 2（第 1 步）。
  2. 用哪个模型？用 ResNet-50（第 2 步）。
  3. 用哪种纠错算法挑要补标的帧？用 jump。三种算法没有明显差距，jump 略微领先（第 3 步）。

中间的逻辑：为什么换了标准还沿用 ResNet-50 和 batch 2

结论：第一阶段选出的 ResNet-50 和 batch 2 继续用，没有在新定义下重选。理由分三步。

  1. 第一阶段的三项比较本身是公平的：当时这个视频的全部训练帧和测试帧都按同一种标法（核对见第 4 步），所以 batch size 之间、模型之间、纠错算法之间的比较成立。
  2. 2026 年 9 月 20 日明确点位定义后变的是点标在哪里（逐点对照见第 4 步）：瞳孔上点从"看得见的瞳孔的最高处"改为"瞳孔椭圆真实的最高点"，上移约 50 px；瞳孔左、右两点从"看得见的瞳孔最靠左、最靠右的位置"改为椭圆横轴的两个端点；两个眼角从阴影和反光的内侧改到外侧，各外移 40–50 px。瞳孔下点和上下眼睑点没有变。图像、任务和训练方式都没有变。
  3. batch size 和模型的选择取决于图像和训练方式，不取决于点标在哪里，所以这两项选择不受影响。

这是一个判断，不是实验结果：没有按明确后的定义重新比较过 batch size 和模型。

第二阶段的结论（第 4–11 步）

前提：把瞳孔看成椭圆，上点标在椭圆真实的最高点；被眼睑挡住时，由标注的人按椭圆估计它的位置。

  1. 同一个视频标多少帧够？约 80 帧以内就饱和：同一个 5 分钟视频按 2026 年 9 月 20 日明确的点位定义重新标注后重做，20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 帧的误差是 12.9 / 13.0 / 12.0 / 13.1 / 12.3 px，没有随帧数下降；之后每个视频的平台点也都不超过 80 帧（第 5 步）。第一阶段在同一个视频上从 80 帧加到 300 帧，误差也没有下降（第 3 步）。
  2. 换一只新老鼠怎么办？要重新标，标 20 帧后误差就降到约 10 px（第 5 步）。
    • 第二只老鼠（模型只见过一只老鼠）：不标时误差 208 px，标 20 帧后 10.4 px。
    • 第三只老鼠（模型已见过两只老鼠）：不标时 26 px，标 20 帧后 9.2 px。
    • 模型见过的老鼠越多，新老鼠不标时的起点误差越小，但标 20 帧仍然需要。
  3. 同一只老鼠之后的每个视频要标多少帧？同一只老鼠之后的 16 个视频里，14 个标 0–40 帧就到平台（0 帧 2 个、20 帧 6 个、40 帧 6 个），视频 14 需要 60 帧，视频 9 需要 80 帧（第 5 步）。
  4. 20 帧已经接近饱和，第一批能不能减到 5 帧或 10 帧？不能取代 20 帧：19 个视频里 5 帧够用的只有 5 个、10 帧 7 个，事先无法判断是哪些，而且结果取决于正好取到哪几帧。这里比的是第一批的大小（从已标的 20 帧里取 5 帧或 10 帧），不是每步 5 帧的流程（第 6 步）。
  5. 加入新视频会不会让旧视频变差？不会（第 7 步）。
  6. 模型给出的哪些点准，置信度能不能信？瞳孔上点最不准（误差中位数 10.2 px，其余 7 个点 3.5–6.0 px）；每个点都是置信度越低误差越大，但瞳孔上点即使置信度高也有 8.3 px（第 8 步）。
  7. 瞳孔面积怎么算？按 2026 年 9 月 20 日明确的定义，瞳孔四个点都是椭圆端点，在 19 个视频的 940 帧测试帧上，四点法的面积误差中位数是 5.4%，三点法是 8.4%；视频 4 和 8 上面积仍然不准（第 9 步）。
  8. 眨眼怎么判断？定义：非睁眼 = 眼睛闭上或眼睑盖住部分瞳孔。这里的分析是静态按帧的，眨眼的动态另做。判据：每只老鼠一个像素阈值，上下眼睑距离低于它判非睁眼，Pluto 147.5 px、Terra 150.5 px。阈值在 327 帧判断帧（标注者只看原图逐帧判睁开 / 非睁眼）上选出，准确率 Pluto 94%、Terra 89%。模型的眼睑距离在 949 张测试帧上和标注只差 2 px。9 个视频（3、4、8–12、15、18）有 20–79% 的帧低于阈值，半掩是那些录像的常态，眨眼是其中的子集（第 10 步）。

Task: DeepLabCut finds 8 points on every frame of a mouse-eye video: pupil top, bottom, left, right; upper and lower eyelid; nasal and temporal eye corner. From these we get pupil size, eye opening and blinks, to be aligned with brain recordings later.

Main question: On a new video, how many frames must a person label before the model is good enough?

The work has two phases, separated by the day the keypoint definitions were made explicit (2026-09-20). Phase 1 used the earlier definitions; only three choices that do not depend on the definitions are kept from it: batch size, model and error-correction algorithm. All other conclusions come from phase 2, which starts again from the same 5-minute video under the explicit definitions. Error numbers of the two phases cannot be compared directly.

Conclusions of phase 1 (steps 1–3)

Premise: pupil top is placed at the highest point of the visible pupil, the part not hidden by the eyelid.

  1. Which batch size? 2. Batches 2, 4, 8 and 16 cannot be told apart; 2 has the lowest validation loss on the 20 validation frames (step 1).
  2. Which model? ResNet-50 (step 2).
  3. Which error-correction algorithm picks the frames to label next? jump. The three algorithms show no clear gap; jump is slightly ahead (step 3).

The logic in between: why ResNet-50 and batch 2 were kept after the standard changed

Conclusion: ResNet-50 and batch 2, chosen in phase 1, are kept; they were not re-selected under the new definitions. Three steps of reasoning.

  1. The three comparisons of phase 1 were fair in themselves: all training and test frames of that video were labeled the same way at the time (checked in step 4), so the comparisons between batch sizes, between models and between error-correction algorithms hold.
  2. What changed on 2026-09-20 is where the points are placed (point-by-point comparison in step 4): pupil top moved from "the highest point of the visible pupil" to "the true top of the pupil ellipse", about 50 px higher; pupil left and right moved from "the leftmost and rightmost visible points of the pupil" to the two ends of the horizontal axis of the ellipse; the two corners moved from the inner side of the shadow and of the reflections to the outer side, 40–50 px outwards each. Pupil bottom and the two eyelid points did not change. The images, the task and the training procedure did not change.
  3. The choice of batch size and model depends on the images and the training procedure, not on where the points are placed, so both choices are unaffected.

This is a judgment, not an experimental result: batch size and model were not compared again under the explicit definitions.

Conclusions of phase 2 (steps 4–11)

Premise: the pupil is treated as an ellipse and pupil top is placed at the true top of that ellipse; where the eyelid hides it, the labeler estimates its position from the ellipse.

  1. How many labels are enough within one video? It saturates within about 80 labels: the same 5-minute video, relabeled under the keypoint definitions made explicit on 2026-09-20 and redone, gives 12.9 / 13.0 / 12.0 / 13.1 / 12.3 px at 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 labels, with no decrease; and no later video has a plateau point above 80 labels (step 5). In phase 1 the same video showed no decrease from 80 to 300 labels either (step 3).
  2. What about a new mouse? It needs its own labels; after 20 of them the error is down to about 10 px (step 5).
    • Second mouse (the model had seen one mouse): error 208 px with no labels, 10.4 px after 20.
    • Third mouse (the model had seen two mice): 26 px with no labels, 9.2 px after 20.
    • The more mice the model has seen, the smaller the starting error on a new mouse without labels; the 20 labels are still needed.
  3. How many labels does each later video of the same mouse need? Of the 16 later videos of the same mouse, 14 reach the plateau with 0–40 labels (0 labels: 2, 20: 6, 40: 6); video 14 needs 60 and video 9 needs 80 (step 5).
  4. 20 labels already come close to saturation; could the first batch be cut to 5 or 10? They cannot replace 20: 5 labels are enough on only 5 of the 19 videos and 10 labels on 7, one cannot tell in advance which, and the result depends on which frames happen to be taken. What is compared is the size of the first batch (5 or 10 taken out of the 20 already labeled), not a procedure of 5 labels per step (step 6).
  5. Do new videos make old ones worse? No (step 7).
  6. Which points are accurate, and can the confidence be trusted? Pupil top is the least accurate (median error 10.2 px; the other 7 points 3.5–6.0 px); for every point a lower confidence goes with a larger error, but pupil top has 8.3 px even at high confidence (step 8).
  7. How to compute the pupil area? Under the definitions made explicit on 2026-09-20 all four pupil points are ellipse endpoints, and on the 940 test frames of 19 videos the four-point area has a median error of 5.4% against 8.4% for the three-point rule; on videos 4 and 8 the area is still not accurate (step 9).
  8. How to detect blinks? Definition: not open = the eye is closed or the lid covers part of the pupil. The analysis here is static, frame by frame; blink dynamics are a separate analysis. Criterion: one pixel threshold per mouse, not open when the eyelid distance is below it, Pluto 147.5 px, Terra 150.5 px. The threshold is chosen on 327 judged frames (the labeler judged open / not open from the raw frame alone), 94% (Pluto) and 89% (Terra) accurate. The model's eyelid distance is within 2 px of the labels on 949 test frames. In 9 videos (3, 4, 8–12, 15, 18) 20–79% of the frames are below the threshold: a half-covered eye is the resting state of those recordings, and blinks are a subset (step 10).

指标怎么算

每个视频留出一组人工标注的测试帧，不参与训练，也不参与任何选择。模型对每一张测试帧给出 8 个点的坐标，坐标是原始视频画面上的像素（928 × 736，不缩放）。模型给出的每个点都参与计算，不按置信度筛掉；人没有标的点（例如闭眼帧里的瞳孔点）不参与计算。

  1. 单个点的误差：第 i 帧第 k 个点，模型预测 (x^ik,y^ik)，人工标注 (xik,yik)，误差是两点之间的直线距离：eik=(x^ik−xik)2+(y^ik−yik)2
  2. 中位帧 RMSE（第 3 步起使用的主要指标）：先对每一帧算这一帧里各点误差的均方根，RMSEi=1Ki∑keik2其中 Ki 是这一帧里人标了的点数（最多 8）；再取所有测试帧 RMSEi 的中位数。
  3. 全部关键点合在一起的 RMSE（第 1、2 步使用）：把所有测试帧、所有点的误差放在一起算一次均方根，RMSE=1N∑i,keik2其中 N 是人标了的点的总数（100 帧 × 8 点，最多 800）。少数错得很远的点会把它拉高，所以后来改用中位帧 RMSE。
  4. 其他几个数：“第 90 百分位”是 RMSEi 的第 90 百分位数；“误差超过 50 px 的测试帧”是 RMSEi > 50 的帧所占比例；“置信度 ≥ 0.6 的点”是人标了的点里模型置信度不低于 0.6 的比例。

数字越小越好。作为参照：按现在的点位定义，眼睛宽度（两个眼角之间的距离，各视频测试帧人工标注的中位数）在 363–497 px 之间，所以 10 px 约为眼宽的 2–3%，50 px 约为 10–14%。计算脚本都在仓库里：第 1、2 步是 v1_old_pupil_top/batch-size-selection/scripts/evaluate_eye_test_set.py 和 model-selection/scripts/ 里的同名脚本，第 3 步是 active-learning-jump-selection/scripts/round_train_pipeline.py，第 5 步起是 v2_new_pupil_top_video_scaling/scripts/scale_step.py；每次打分的结果写在各 results/ 文件夹的 csv 表里。每一步用的视频、数据、模型和指标，写在那一步开头的“基本信息”里。

训练配置（第 1、2 步选定，两个阶段都使用）：ResNet-50，batch 2。每个模型都从 ImageNet 预训练权重开始重新训练，不接着我们自己之前的模型继续训。

How the metrics are computed

Each video has a set of human-labeled test frames that are never used for training or for any choice. For every test frame the model gives the coordinates of the 8 points, in pixels of the original video frame (928 × 736, no rescaling). Every predicted point enters the calculation, none is dropped for low confidence; points the person did not label (for example the pupil points of a closed-eye frame) are left out.

  1. Error of one point: for point k of frame i, with prediction (x^ik,y^ik) and human label (xik,yik), the error is the straight-line distance between the two: eik=(x^ik−xik)2+(y^ik−yik)2
  2. Median frame RMSE (the main metric from step 3 on): first the root mean square of the point errors within each frame, RMSEi=1Ki∑keik2where Ki is the number of labeled points in that frame (at most 8); then the median of RMSEi over all test frames.
  3. RMSE over all keypoints (used in steps 1 and 2): one root mean square over the errors of all points of all test frames, RMSE=1N∑i,keik2where N is the total number of labeled points (100 frames × 8 points, at most 800). A few points that are far off pull it up, which is why the median frame RMSE was used later.
  4. The other numbers: "90th percentile" is the 90th percentile of RMSEi; "test frames above 50 px" is the share of frames with RMSEi > 50; "points with confidence ≥ 0.6" is the share of labeled points whose model confidence is at least 0.6.

Lower is better. For scale: under the present keypoint definitions the eye width (distance between the two corners, median of the human labels on each video's test frames) is 363–497 px, so 10 px is about 2–3% of the eye width and 50 px about 10–14%. The scoring scripts are in the repository: v1_old_pupil_top/batch-size-selection/scripts/evaluate_eye_test_set.py and the script of the same name under model-selection/scripts/ for steps 1 and 2, active-learning-jump-selection/scripts/round_train_pipeline.py for step 3, and v2_new_pupil_top_video_scaling/scripts/scale_step.py from step 5 on; every score is written to the csv tables of the results/ folders. The video, data, model and metric of every step are listed in the "Basic information" table at its start.

Training recipe (chosen in steps 1 and 2, used in both phases): ResNet-50, batch 2. Every model is trained anew from ImageNet-pretrained weights; it never continues from one of our earlier models.

What was done:With the reviewed labels (first 80 frames train, last 20 validate), one run each at batch 2, 4, 8, 16 (100 epochs). The selection rule was fixed before looking at results: lowest internal validation loss. The final-minute test frames are report-only.

Conclusion:Batches 2, 4, 8 and 16 cannot be told apart: one run each, the validation loss on the 20 validation frames differs by only 7%, and the error on the 100 test frames is 21.1–23.6 px (RMSE over all keypoints) or 18.5–20.1 px (median frame RMSE). Batch 2 is taken as the one with the lowest validation loss and is used in every later step.

Conclusion:ResNet-50; all later experiments use ResNet-50 with batch 2.
  • Reasons: lowest test error by both metrics: RMSE over all keypoints 20.1 px, median frame RMSE 18.4 px; best use of confidence (514 of 800 points with confidence ≥ 0.6, against 75–375 for the other models), which helps when confidence is used later to detect blinks and tracking loss; cheapest training (1.7 h, against 3.7 h for HRNet-W32, the model with a similar error). 3选纠错算法：jump（主动学习）Error-correction algorithm: jump (active learning)

基本信息Basic information

视频Video第一只老鼠的 5 分钟眼睛视频（60 帧/秒，共 18,000 帧）。前 4 分钟用于挑帧和训练，最后 1 分钟只用于测试。The 5-minute eye video of the first mouse (60 fps, 18,000 frames). The first 4 minutes are used for frame selection and training, the final minute for testing only.

做了什么：选一种纠错算法。模型训练好之后，由算法在视频里找出"模型可能出错的帧"，人只改正这些帧，再重新训练，这种做法叫主动学习。比较三种算法，看哪一种挑出的帧最能降低误差。

流程。从 80 帧起步。每轮：算法在整段视频的预测里挑出可疑帧 → 按画面外观聚成 20 类，每类取一帧 → 人工改正这 20 帧，加进训练集 → 重新训练。一共 11 轮，从 80 帧加到 300 帧。三种算法各走一条线。

三种算法（都是 DeepLabCut 自带的 extract_outlier_frames，输入是模型对整段视频每一帧的预测）：

  • uncertain：8 个点里任何一个点的置信度低于 0.6 的帧。
  • jump：任何一个点相对上一帧移动超过 20 px 的帧。
  • fitting：对每个点的轨迹拟合一个时间序列模型（ARIMA，只用置信度高的帧拟合），8 个点偏离拟合位置的平均距离超过 20 px 的帧。

What was done:Choose an error-correction algorithm. Once a model is trained, an algorithm searches the video for frames where the model is probably wrong, a person corrects only those frames and the model is retrained; this is active learning. Three algorithms were compared to see whose picks reduce the error most.

Procedure. Start from 80 labels. Each round: the algorithm flags suspect frames in the prediction of the whole video → the flagged frames are clustered into 20 groups by image appearance, one frame per group → a person corrects these 20 frames and they are added to the training set → retrain. 11 rounds, from 80 to 300 labels. Each algorithm runs as its own branch.

The three algorithms (all built into DeepLabCut, extract_outlier_frames; input = the model's prediction of every frame of the video):

  • uncertain: frames in which any of the 8 points has confidence below 0.6.
  • jump: frames in which any point moved more than 20 px from the previous frame.
  • fitting: frames in which the 8 points deviate on average by more than 20 px from a time-series model (ARIMA, fitted on the confident frames only) of each point's trajectory.

结论：这一步有两个结论：纠错算法选 jump；在这个视频上，标注到约 80 帧就饱和。

  1. 纠错算法选 jump，之后的实验统一用它挑帧。在这次实验里 jump 的误差最低：11 轮的平均中位误差是 jump 16.4 px、uncertain 17.0 px、fitting 17.0 px，11 轮里有 8 轮 jump 最低。领先幅度约 0.6 px，小于同一设置重新训练一次时的波动（0.3–2 px），所以我们认为这几种纠错算法差距不大，jump 略微领先。
  2. 关于 jump 的阈值：这一步用固定的 20 px。第 5 步起改为该视频眼宽中位数的 3%，算法本身不变，让眼宽不同的视频用同一个相对标准（各视频眼宽 363–497 px，阈值约 11–15 px）。按现在的眼角定义，这个 5 分钟视频眼宽 497 px，20 px 约为 4%，所以第 5 步的阈值比这一步略严。
  3. 在这个视频上，约 80 帧就饱和。从 80 帧加到 300 帧，三条线的中位误差一直在 15.7–17.5 px 之间，没有下降。
    • 这个结果用的是 2026 年 9 月 20 日之前的点位定义。
    • 2026 年 9 月 20 日明确点位定义之后，在同一个视频上重新标注、重做了一遍，结论一致：20、40、60、80、100 帧的误差都在 12–13 px 之间，没有随帧数下降（第 5 步，视频 0）。明确的内容见第 4 步，主要是瞳孔上点从“看得见的瞳孔的最高处”改为“瞳孔椭圆真实的最高点”，两个眼角明确为睑缘延长线的交点。
    • 两套定义下的误差数值不能直接比较，一致的是“约 80 帧以内就饱和”。

Conclusion:This step has two conclusions: jump is chosen as the error-correction algorithm, and on this video about 80 labels saturate.

  1. jump is the error-correction algorithm and picks the frames in all later experiments. In this experiment jump has the lowest error: the mean of the median error over the 11 rounds is 16.4 px for jump, 17.0 px for uncertain and 17.0 px for fitting, and jump is the lowest in 8 of the 11 rounds. The lead is about 0.6 px, smaller than the variation when one setting is trained again (0.3–2 px), so we regard the three error-correction algorithms as close, with jump slightly ahead.
  2. On the jump threshold: this step used a fixed 20 px. From step 5 on it is 3% of the video's median eye width; the algorithm is unchanged, and videos with different eye widths share one relative standard (eye widths 363–497 px, threshold about 11–15 px). Under the present corner definition this 5-minute video's eye is 497 px wide, so 20 px is about 4%: the step-5 threshold is slightly stricter than this one.
  3. On this video about 80 labels saturate. From 80 to 300 labels the median error of all three branches stays between 15.7 and 17.5 px, with no decrease.
    • This result uses the keypoint definitions from before 2026-09-20.
    • After the definitions were made explicit on 2026-09-20, the same video was relabeled and the experiment repeated, with the same conclusion: the error stays between 12 and 13 px at 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100 labels and does not fall with the number of labels (step 5, video 0). What was made explicit is described in step 4; mainly, pupil top moved from "the highest point of the visible pupil" to "the true top of the pupil ellipse", and the two corners became the crossing of the continued eyelid margins.
    • The error values under the two sets of definitions cannot be compared directly; what agrees is that the video saturates within about 80 labels.

4点位定义的明确（2026 年 9 月 20 日起）：瞳孔按椭圆标，眼角取睑缘延长线的交点Keypoint definitions made explicit (from 2026-09-20): pupil as an ellipse, corners at the crossing of the eyelid margins

做了什么：把 8 个点各自标在哪里写清楚，并按新的写法把之前的视频全部重新标注。从这一步起所有模型和数字都用这套定义。完整的定义、例图和容易标错的地方在仓库的 keypoint_definitions/ 文件夹里。下面按点位依次说明，每一项后面紧跟对应的图。

What was done:Where each of the 8 points is placed was written down, and all earlier videos were relabeled accordingly. From this step on every model and number uses these definitions. The full definitions, example figures and the common mistakes are in the folder keypoint_definitions/ of the repository. The points are described one group at a time below, each followed by its figure.

  • 瞳孔上、下、左、右（图 4-2）：把瞳孔看作一个椭圆，四个点是椭圆两条轴的四个端点。被上眼睑或毛挡住的部分按椭圆补全，所以瞳孔上点常常落在上眼睑点的上方，也就是标在被眼睑盖住的位置上。图 4-2 里红点是现在的位置；黄色叉是 2026 年 9 月 20 日之前的位置：标在看得见的瞳孔最靠上、下、左、右的地方，没有按椭圆去拟合。瞳孔边界取变暗区域的外缘；边界出现两层轮廓时取左边那层；黑色核心外面较亮的一圈也算瞳孔。
  • Pupil top, bottom, left, right (Fig. 4-2): the pupil is treated as an ellipse and the four points are the ends of its two axes. Parts hidden by the upper eyelid or by fur are completed with the ellipse, so the pupil-top point often lies above the upper-eyelid point, that is, at a position covered by the lid. In Fig. 4-2 the red dots are the present positions; the yellow crosses are the positions before 2026-09-20: at the topmost, bottommost, leftmost and rightmost visible points of the pupil, without fitting an ellipse. The pupil boundary is the outer edge of the darkened region; where the edge looks doubled the left contour is used; the brighter band around the dark core counts as pupil.
  • 上眼睑、下眼睑（见上面的图 4-1）：眼皮和眼球的分界线叫睑缘。上眼睑点是上睑缘的中点，下眼睑点是下睑缘的中点。
  • Upper and lower eyelid (Fig. 4-1 above): the line between the eyelid skin and the eyeball is the eyelid margin. The upper-eyelid point is the midpoint of the upper margin, the lower-eyelid point the midpoint of the lower margin.
  • 左眼角（鼻侧，内眦；图 4-3 第二行）：眼角处有一片阴影，往下越来越窄，一直延伸到下眼睑附近。取阴影外侧的边作为左下方的睑缘（红色实线），把它和上睑缘各自延长（红色虚线），两条延长线的交点（红圈）就是左眼角，青色点是标注的位置。不取的画法（黄色点线和黄色叉）：沿着阴影内侧的边画，交点落在阴影里面。图中斜线区域就是两种画法之间的阴影。
  • 右眼角（颞侧，外眦；图 4-3 第三行）：右侧有几条反光亮带，亮带下面接着一段阴影。把下睑缘沿外层那条线向上延伸（红色虚线），绕到反光的右侧，与上睑缘相交的点（红圈）就是右眼角，青色点是标注的位置。取外侧的原因：皮毛不会反光，所以右侧这些反光亮带仍然在眼睛内部，眼角应该在反光的外面。不取的画法（黄色点线和黄色叉）：沿着反光的内沿和它下面阴影的内边缘画，交点落在紧挨反光的内层线上。也不标在反光亮带上。
  • Left corner (nasal, medial canthus; Fig. 4-3, second row): there is a shadow at this corner; it gets narrower downwards and continues to near the lower eyelid. The outer border of the shadow is taken as the lower-left margin (solid red); it and the upper margin are each continued (dashed red), and the crossing of the two continuations (red circle) is the left corner; the cyan dot is the labeled position. Not used (dotted yellow line and yellow cross): following the inner border of the shadow, which puts the crossing inside the shadow. The hatched area is the shadow between the two constructions.
  • Right corner (temporal, lateral canthus; Fig. 4-3, third row): there are several bright reflections on this side, with a stretch of shadow below them. The lower margin is continued upwards along the outer line (dashed red), around the right of the reflections, and the point where it meets the upper margin (red circle) is the right corner; the cyan dot is the labeled position. Why the outer side: fur does not reflect, so the bright reflections on the right are still inside the eye and the corner must lie outside them. Not used (dotted yellow line and yellow cross): following the inner edge of the reflection and of the shadow below it, which puts the crossing on the inner line next to the reflection. The corner is not placed on a reflection either.
  • 看不清的点：所有 8 个点都一样，看得清楚就标；不能准确判断位置时，这个点留空不标。
  • Points that cannot be seen: the same for all 8 points: a point is labeled when it can be seen clearly and left empty when its position cannot be judged accurately.

5.1 每一批 20 帧是怎么挑出来的（算法）5.1 How each batch of 20 frames is picked (algorithm)

脚本：v2_new_pupil_top_video_scaling/scripts/select_frames.py。下面的数字都是脚本里固定的参数，照着做可以得到同样的帧。

（一）先把视频分成三段

  1. 测试段：视频最后 10%（不少于 60 秒）。在这一段里按时间等间隔取 50 帧，作为测试帧。
  2. 验证段：测试段之前的 10%（不少于 60 秒）。等间隔取 20 帧，作为验证帧。
  3. 候选池：验证段之前的全部帧（约前 80%；19.9 分钟的视频是 57158 帧）。训练用的帧只从这里挑。验证段两侧各去掉 2 秒，避免三段之间有几乎相同的相邻帧。

（二）第 1 批：按画面外观聚类（不用任何模型）

  1. 在候选池里每 5 帧取 1 帧。
  2. 给每一帧算一个“外观指纹”：转成灰度图，缩小到 128 × 96；用标准差 1 px 的高斯模糊图减去标准差 6 px 的高斯模糊图（留下边缘和形状，去掉缓慢的明暗变化）；再缩小到 32 × 24，展开成 768 个数；减去均值、除以标准差。
  3. 对这些指纹做 k-means 聚类，分成 20 类（scikit-learn 的 KMeans，n_clusters=20, n_init=10, max_iter=300, random_state=42）。
  4. 每一类取离类中心最近的那一帧（欧氏距离），共 20 帧。从帧数最多的类开始取。
  5. 间隔要求：取的帧之间、以及和已经标过的帧之间，至少相隔 1 秒；不满足的跳过。如果因此不足 20 帧，已取的帧保留，把类数增加到 30、40、…（最多 80）重新聚类，新的类中心帧按类的大小从大到小、满足间隔的依次取，只补到 20 帧为止。

（三）第 2 批起：先用 jump 规则找候选帧，再按外观聚类

  1. 用上一步训练出的模型预测整段视频的每一帧。
  2. 定阈值（一个像素数，每个视频一个）：眼宽 = 每一帧两个眼角的距离在整段视频的中位数；阈值 = 眼宽 × 3%，约 11–15 px。眼宽只用来定这个数，不参与筛选。
  3. 算每一帧的位移：8 个点各自相对上一帧移动了多少像素，取 8 个里最大的一个。
  4. 判据：8 个点里最大的位移 > 眼宽 × 3%，这一帧就是候选帧（一个视频的眼宽是一个定值，所以阈值整段视频不变；任何一个点跳得大都算）。候选帧多于 12000 帧时，等间隔只保留约 12000 帧（为了聚类的计算量）；少于 40 帧时改用位移最大的 2000 帧。
  5. 对候选帧做和第 1 批完全相同的步骤：算外观指纹、k-means 分 20 类、每类取离中心最近的一帧、相隔至少 1 秒；不足 20 帧时把类数加到 30、40、…重新聚类，按类的大小顺序只补差额。
  6. 如果还不足 20 帧（模型已经很好、候选帧很少且挤在几秒内时会发生），按位移从大到小从候选帧里补，仍要求相隔 1 秒。

所以每一批都是“一类一帧”：jump 规则决定去哪里找（模型不稳的帧），聚类保证挑出的 20 帧画面各不相同，而不是 20 张几乎一样的帧。

Script: v2_new_pupil_top_video_scaling/scripts/select_frames.py. All numbers below are fixed parameters of the script; following them gives the same frames.

(a) The video is first split into three segments

  1. Test segment: the final 10% of the video (at least 60 s). 50 frames evenly spaced in time are the test frames.
  2. Validation segment: the 10% before it (at least 60 s). 20 evenly spaced frames are the validation frames.
  3. Pool: all frames before the validation segment (about the first 80%; 57158 frames for a 19.9-min video). Training frames are picked from the pool only. 2 s are removed on both sides of the validation segment so that the three segments share no near-identical neighbouring frames.

(b) Batch 1: clustering by image appearance (no model)

  1. Every 5th frame of the pool is taken.
  2. An "appearance fingerprint" is computed for each frame: greyscale, downscaled to 128 × 96; a Gaussian blur with sigma 1 px minus a Gaussian blur with sigma 6 px (keeps edges and shape, removes slow lighting changes); downscaled to 32 × 24 and flattened to 768 numbers; mean subtracted and divided by the standard deviation.
  3. k-means on the fingerprints with 20 clusters (scikit-learn KMeans, n_clusters=20, n_init=10, max_iter=300, random_state=42).
  4. From each cluster the frame closest to the cluster centre (Euclidean distance) is taken, 20 frames in total, starting with the largest cluster.
  5. Spacing: picked frames must be at least 1 s apart from each other and from every frame labeled earlier; frames that violate this are skipped. If fewer than 20 remain, the frames already taken are kept, the number of clusters is raised to 30, 40, … (at most 80) and re-clustered, and the new centre frames are taken in order of cluster size, those that satisfy the spacing, only until 20 are reached.

(c) From batch 2 on: candidates by the jump rule, then clustering by appearance

  1. The model of the previous step predicts every frame of the whole video.
  2. Threshold (one pixel number per video): eye width = median over the video of the distance between the two corners in each frame; threshold = 3% of it, about 11–15 px. The eye width only sets this number and plays no part in the selection.
  3. Displacement of each frame: how many pixels each of the 8 points moved from the previous frame; the largest of the 8.
  4. The rule: largest displacement of the 8 points > eye width × 3% → the frame is a candidate (a video's eye width is one fixed number, so the threshold is constant over the video; any one point jumping far counts). With more than 12000 candidates only about 12000 are kept, evenly spaced (to bound the clustering cost); with fewer than 40, the 2000 frames with the largest displacement are used instead.
  5. The candidates go through exactly the steps of batch 1: fingerprints, k-means with 20 clusters, the frame closest to each centre, at least 1 s apart; if fewer than 20, the number of clusters is raised to 30, 40, … and re-clustered, topping up only the shortfall in order of cluster size.
  6. If there are still fewer than 20 (this happens when the model is already good and the few candidates are bunched within a few seconds), the batch is filled from the candidates in order of decreasing jump, still 1 s apart.

Every batch is therefore "one frame per cluster": the jump rule decides where to look (frames on which the model is unstable), and the clustering makes sure the 20 picked frames look different from each other instead of being 20 nearly identical frames.

做了什么：从每个视频已经标好的第一批 20 帧里取 5 帧或 10 帧，其余条件和该视频第 1 步完全相同，重新训练并打分，不需要新的标注。帧数这么少时，结果很受“正好取到哪几帧”的影响，为了避免偶然性，5 帧和 10 帧各取两组不同的帧，分别训练，记为 5a、5b 和 10a、10b；图上的色带就是两组结果之间的范围。

5 帧和 10 帧具体怎么取（算法）。脚本：v2_new_pupil_top_video_scaling/scripts/make_label_subsets.py。只用第一批已经标好的 20 帧和当时聚类留下的结果，不用任何模型，也不需要新的标注。

  1. 第一批的 20 帧来自 20 个外观类（第 5 步 5.1 节的第 1 批算法），每一类有一个类中心（768 个数的外观指纹）和这一类的帧数。
  2. 把这 20 个类中心再做一次 k-means，合并成 5 组（取 5 帧时）或 10 组（取 10 帧时）。每个类中心按它那一类的帧数加权（scikit-learn 的 KMeans，n_clusters=5 或 10, n_init=20, random_state=0）。
  3. 已标的 20 帧各自按外观指纹归到离它最近的那一组。
  4. a 组：每一组里取离组中心最近的那一帧已标帧。b 组：每一组里取离组中心第二近的那一帧（这一组只有一帧已标帧时，仍取那一帧）。
  5. 如果有的组取到了重复的帧、凑不够 5 帧或 10 帧，就从没用到的已标帧里，按离任一组中心的距离从近到远补足。

所以 5 帧和 10 帧也是“每一种画面形态取一帧”，只是分得更粗；a 和 b 是同一种分法下的两组不同的帧（同一组里最近和第二近的帧），19 个视频里 5a 和 5b 没有共同的帧（只有视频 12 和 14 各有 1 帧相同），10a 和 10b 有 2–5 帧相同。

How exactly the 5 and 10 labels are taken (algorithm). Script: v2_new_pupil_top_video_scaling/scripts/make_label_subsets.py. It uses only the 20 labeled frames of the first batch and the clustering saved at the time; no model and no new labeling.

  1. The 20 frames of the first batch come from 20 appearance clusters (batch-1 algorithm of section 5.1 in step 5); each cluster has a centre (a 768-number fingerprint) and a number of frames.
  2. The 20 cluster centres are clustered again by k-means into 5 groups (for 5 labels) or 10 groups (for 10 labels), each centre weighted by the number of frames in its cluster (scikit-learn KMeans, n_clusters=5 or 10, n_init=20, random_state=0).
  3. Each of the 20 labeled frames is assigned by its fingerprint to the nearest group.
  4. Subset a: from each group, the labeled frame closest to the group centre. Subset b: from each group, the second closest (the same frame again when the group holds only one labeled frame).
  5. If groups returned the same frame and fewer than 5 or 10 distinct frames result, the subset is filled from the unused labeled frames in order of increasing distance to any group centre.
  6. This gives four lists: 5a, 5b, 10a, 10b. In training, the video's own 20 labels are replaced by the 5 or 10 listed frames; the carried labels of the earlier videos, the validation frames (20), the test frames (50), the model and the training settings are exactly those of that video's step 1 (scale_step.py --step 1 --subset list-file).

So the 5 and 10 labels are also "one frame per kind of appearance", only with a coarser grouping; a and b are two different sets of frames under the same grouping (the closest and the second closest frame of each group) In the 19 videos 5a and 5b have no frame in common (except 1 frame each on videos 12 and 14), and 10a and 10b share 2–5 frames.

8.1 模型训练饱和后，如何有效选出不可信的帧：比较三种纠错算法的适用场景8.1 After training has saturated, how to pick out unreliable frames: the three outlier algorithms by use case

目的。第 3 步比较了三种纠错算法（jump、uncertain、fitting）哪一种最适合在主动学习里挑下一批要标注的帧，选了 jump。这里的问题不同：模型训练饱和、对整段视频预测完之后，哪些帧的预测不可信。所以把同样三种算法再比一次：量每种算法标出的帧里有多少真的不可信、真的不可信的帧里有多少被标出，再按三种用途（人工复核、挑训练帧、剔除不可信帧）分别看哪一种合适。

用的帧。每个视频（视频 0–18）的 50 帧测试帧和 20 帧验证帧，共 19 × 70 = 1330 帧。这些帧按时间等间隔取，不是任何算法挑的；每帧都有人工标注；模型没有用它们训练。

真值和两档不可信。每帧用该视频平台点的模型算帧 RMSE：预测的 8 个点和标注的 8 个点之间的距离（公式见页面开头）。帧 RMSE 越大，这一帧的预测离标注越远。按它分两档：非常不可信 = 帧 RMSE 超过 50 px（约眼宽的 10–14%，预测点离标注位置很远），1330 帧里有 32 帧（2.4%）；轻微不可信 = 超过 20 px（约眼宽的 4–6%，预测点在标注位置附近但有偏差），有 155 帧（11.7%，含上面的 32 帧）。50 px 和 20 px 这两个界限是我们定的，没有用数据选。

三种算法：每种算什么、阈值多少、阈值从哪来。每种算法对每一帧算出一个数，只用模型的预测，不用标注；这个数越过它的阈值，就把这一帧标为不可信。

  • jump。算什么：8 个点里相对上一帧移动最远的那个点的位移，除以该视频眼宽的中位数。阈值：0.03，即眼宽的 3%，在各视频上约 11–15 px。来源：DLC 自带的 jump 用固定的 20 px，第 3 步用的就是它；第 5 步起改为眼宽的 3%，使眼宽不同的视频用同一个相对标准；这里用的就是这条规则。
  • uncertain。算什么：8 个点里最低的置信度。阈值：0.6。来源：DLC 配置里 pcutoff 的默认值，DLC 画图和过滤时把置信度低于它的点当不可信。DLC 自带的 uncertain 算法用的是 0.01；在这 1330 帧上，0.01 一帧都标不出来，所以没有用。
  • fitting。算什么：对每个点的 x 坐标和 y 坐标，各自在整段视频上拟合 SARIMAX(3,0,1) 时间序列模型（置信度低于 0.01 的点当缺失，序列先减去均值）；一个点的偏差 = 模型预测的位置和时间序列一步预测的位置之间的距离；fitting = 8 个点偏差的平均。阈值：20 px。来源：DLC 的默认值，没有改。算法按 DLC 文档用 statsmodels 重新实现，因为 DLC 的函数只输出整段视频的标记，不输出每帧的偏差。

另外两条规则。（4）uncertain 平均：8 个点置信度的平均值低于 t。t 不是默认值，是在这 1330 帧上选的：把 19 个视频中的一个拿出来，用其余 18 个视频的 1260 帧选出让轻微不可信帧（超过 20 px）的检出率减误报率最大的 t，再用这个 t 判定拿出来的那个视频的 70 帧；19 个视频轮流拿出一次，所以每一帧都是用没有见过它所在视频的 t 判定的。在全部 1330 帧上选出的 t 是 0.79。（5）jump 或 uncertain：两条里任一条标出就标出。图和表里五条规则的名字：jump、uncertain（最低置信度 < 0.6）、uncertain 平均（平均置信度 < t）、jump 或 uncertain、fitting。

怎么评。每条规则报两个数：准确率 = 标出的帧里真的不可信的比例；检出率 = 真的不可信的帧里被标出的比例。另外对每种算法算 AUC：把阈值从最小值到最大值逐个试，每个阈值得到一对（误报率，检出率），连成曲线，AUC 是曲线下的面积；1 表示所有不可信帧的数都比可信帧更极端，0.5 表示和随机挑一样。AUC 不依赖阈值。

脚本和结果。scripts/unreliable_frames.py；results/unreliable_frames/（每帧一行的 unreliable_frames_all_videos.csv，每条规则一行的 unreliable_rules_all_videos.csv，每个视频一行的 unreliable_signals_all_videos.csv）。

Purpose. Step 3 compared the three outlier algorithms (jump, uncertain, fitting) as pickers of the next frames to label in active learning and chose jump. The question here is different: once training has saturated and the model has predicted a whole video, which frames are unreliable. So the same three algorithms are compared again: for each, the share of flagged frames that are truly unreliable and the share of truly unreliable frames that are flagged, and then, for three uses (human review, picking training frames, removing unreliable frames), which algorithm suits.

Frames used. The 50 test frames and 20 validation frames of every video (videos 0–18), 19 × 70 = 1330 frames. They are evenly spaced in time, not picked by any algorithm; every frame is human-labeled; no model was trained on them.

Truth and the two levels. For each frame the frame RMSE of the video's plateau-point model: the distance between the 8 predicted and the 8 labeled points (formula at the top of the page). The larger it is, the farther the prediction is from the labels. Two levels: very unreliable = frame RMSE above 50 px (about 10–14% of the eye width; predicted points far from the labeled positions), 32 of the 1330 frames (2.4%); mildly unreliable = above 20 px (about 4–6% of the eye width; predicted points near the labeled positions but offset), 155 frames (11.7%, including the 32). The limits 50 px and 20 px are ours and were not chosen from data.

The three algorithms: what each computes, its threshold, where the threshold comes from. Each algorithm gives every frame one number from the model's predictions alone, without labels; the frame is flagged as unreliable when that number crosses the threshold.

  • jump. Computes: the displacement of the keypoint that moved farthest from the previous frame, divided by the video's median eye width. Threshold: 0.03, i.e. 3% of the eye width, about 11–15 px per video. Origin: DLC's own jump uses a fixed 20 px, which step 3 used; from step 5 on it is 3% of the eye width, so that videos with different eye widths share one relative standard; that is the rule used here.
  • uncertain. Computes: the lowest confidence of the 8 points. Threshold: 0.6. Origin: the default pcutoff of the DLC config, below which DLC treats a point as unreliable when plotting and filtering. DLC's own uncertain algorithm uses 0.01; on these 1330 frames 0.01 flags no frame at all, so it was not used.
  • fitting. Computes: for each keypoint's x and y coordinate a SARIMAX(3,0,1) time-series model fitted to the whole video (points with confidence below 0.01 treated as missing, mean subtracted first); deviation of a keypoint = distance between the model's predicted position and the one-step prediction of the time series; fitting = mean deviation over the 8 keypoints. Threshold: 20 px. Origin: DLC's default, unchanged. The algorithm is re-implemented with statsmodels after DLC's documentation, because DLC's function only outputs the flags for a whole video, not the per-frame deviation.

Two further rules. (4) uncertain mean: the mean confidence of the 8 points below t. t is not a default; it was chosen on these 1330 frames: one of the 19 videos is set aside, on the 1260 frames of the other 18 the t that maximizes recall minus false-alarm rate for the mildly unreliable frames (above 20 px) is chosen, and that t judges the 70 frames of the set-aside video; each of the 19 videos is set aside once, so every frame is judged with a t chosen without its own video. On all 1330 frames the chosen t is 0.79. (5) jump or uncertain: flagged when either of the two flags. The five rules are named in the figure and table as: jump, uncertain (lowest confidence < 0.6), uncertain mean (mean confidence < t), jump or uncertain, fitting.

How they are scored. Two numbers per rule: precision = share of the flagged frames that are truly unreliable; recall = share of the truly unreliable frames that are flagged. In addition an AUC per algorithm: the threshold is moved from the smallest to the largest value, each value gives one pair (false-alarm rate, recall), the pairs form a curve, and the AUC is the area under it; 1 means every unreliable frame has a more extreme value than every reliable frame, 0.5 means no better than random picking. The AUC does not depend on the threshold.

阈值怎么选（图 10-1）。每只老鼠只用自己的判断帧。候选 T 从 60 px 到 300 px，每 1 px 试一次。

每个 T 把帧分成四格：

  • 抓对：真值非睁眼，O < T
  • 漏掉：真值非睁眼，O ≥ T
  • 误报：真值睁开，O < T
  • 对：真值睁开，O ≥ T

三个比例：

  • 检出率 = 抓对 ÷ 真值非睁眼的帧数
  • 误报率 = 误报 ÷ 真值睁开的帧数
  • 一致率（准确率）= (抓对 + 对) ÷ 全部帧数

选法：图里橙线是 检出率 − 误报率，蓝线是一致率。取橙线最高的 T，并列最高的一段取中点。

为什么用橙线不用蓝线：一致率把两类帧混在一起算，哪类多就偏向哪类。极端情况：若 90% 的帧睁开，阈值定到 0、一帧都不标，一致率也有 90%。检出率 − 误报率 两类各算一个比例再相减，不受两类比例影响。这次两类帧数接近（Pluto 77 对 74，Terra 64 对 62），两条线的顶点落在同一个 T。

代入数字：

  • Terra，126 帧（非睁眼 64、睁开 62）。T = 150.5：抓对 59、漏掉 5、误报 9、对 53。检出率 59 ÷ 64 = 92.2%，误报率 9 ÷ 62 = 14.5%，橙线 92.2 − 14.5 = 77.7，一致率 (59 + 53) ÷ 126 = 88.9%。
  • Pluto，151 帧（非睁眼 77、睁开 74）。T = 147.5：抓对 71、漏掉 6、误报 3、对 71。检出率 71 ÷ 77 = 92.2%，误报率 3 ÷ 74 = 4.1%，橙线 92.2 − 4.1 = 88.2，一致率 (71 + 71) ÷ 151 = 94.0%。

How the threshold is chosen (Figure 10-1). Each mouse uses only its own judged frames. Candidate T runs from 60 to 300 px in steps of 1 px.

Each T splits the frames into four cells:

  • hit: truth not open, O < T
  • miss: truth not open, O ≥ T
  • false alarm: truth open, O < T
  • correct rejection: truth open, O ≥ T

Three rates:

  • recall = hits ÷ frames whose truth is not open
  • false-alarm rate = false alarms ÷ frames whose truth is open
  • agreement (accuracy) = (hits + correct rejections) ÷ all frames

Choice: the orange line is recall − false-alarm rate, the blue line the agreement. The T with the highest orange value is taken, the middle of a tied range.

Why orange rather than blue: the agreement pools the two classes and leans toward whichever is more frequent. In the extreme, with 90% of the frames open, a threshold of 0 that flags nothing still agrees 90%. Recall − false-alarm rate takes one rate per class and subtracts, so it does not depend on the class proportions. Here the classes are nearly balanced (Pluto 77 against 74, Terra 64 against 62) and both lines peak at the same T.

With the actual numbers:

  • Terra, 126 frames (64 not open, 62 open). T = 150.5: hits 59, misses 5, false alarms 9, correct rejections 53. Recall 59 ÷ 64 = 92.2%, false-alarm rate 9 ÷ 62 = 14.5%, orange 92.2 − 14.5 = 77.7, agreement (59 + 53) ÷ 126 = 88.9%.
  • Pluto, 151 frames (77 not open, 74 open). T = 147.5: hits 71, misses 6, false alarms 3, correct rejections 71. Recall 71 ÷ 77 = 92.2%, false-alarm rate 3 ÷ 74 = 4.1%, orange 92.2 − 4.1 = 88.2, agreement (71 + 71) ÷ 151 = 94.0%.

12还没有结论的地方Where there is no conclusion yet

  • 不可信帧：怎么把预测不可信的帧找出来、怎么继续降低它们，没有结论。详见第 8 步 8.1 节。
  • 视频 4、5、8 误差偏大（视频 8 在 25 px 以上）。原因是画面模糊，肉眼也难定点，标注者本身的判断就不稳定。整幅画面的清晰度和亮度与其他 Pluto 视频无差别，模糊在瞳孔边缘的局部，还没有量化。
  • 面积：用四点法，全部测试帧上中位误差 5.4%，三点法 8.4%；但 19 个视频里有 4 个（视频 1、2、5、7）三点法更低，视频 4、8 两种算法都不准（43.8%、24.0%），这两点还没有解释。平台点集中在 20–40 帧（20 帧和 40 帧各 7 个视频，0 帧 2 个，60 帧 2 个，80 帧 1 个），但每个点只训练一次、测试集 50 帧，±20 帧内分不出。
  • Unreliable frames: how to find the frames whose prediction cannot be trusted, and how to reduce them further, is unresolved. See step 8, section 8.1.
  • Videos 4, 5 and 8 have larger errors (video 8 above 25 px). The cause is a blurry image: the points are hard to place even by eye, so the labeler's own judgement is unstable. Whole-image sharpness and brightness do not differ from the other Pluto videos; the blur is local, at the pupil edge, and has not been quantified.
  • Area: the four-point rule is used; median error 5.4% on all test frames against 8.4% for the three-point rule. But on 4 of the 19 videos (videos 1, 2, 5, 7) the three-point rule is lower, and on videos 4 and 8 both rules are inaccurate (43.8%, 24.0%); neither is explained. Plateau points cluster at 20–40 labels (20 and 40 labels: 7 videos each; 0 labels: 2; 60 labels: 2; 80 labels: 1), but each point is a single training run on 50 test frames, so differences within ±20 labels cannot be resolved.
