小鼠眼睛关键点追踪：做了什么，结果是什么Mouse eye keypoint tracking: what was done and what came out

Zhiheng Li (University of Minnesota), Kaiwen Sheng (Stanford) · 页面生成时间page built {{BUILT}} · GitHub: ZhihengLi0/mouse-behavior-tracking

任务：用 DeepLabCut 在小鼠眼睛视频的每一帧上找 8 个点：瞳孔上、下、左、右，上眼睑、下眼睑，鼻侧眼角、颞侧眼角。由这 8 个点得到瞳孔大小、眼睛睁开程度和眨眼，之后与脑记录对齐。

核心问题：拿到一个新视频，人要标多少帧，模型才够用？

工作分成两个阶段，以 2026 年 9 月 20 日明确点位定义为界。第一阶段用的是之前的点位定义，只保留三项不依赖点位定义的选择：batch size、模型、纠错算法。其余结论都来自第二阶段，从同一个 5 分钟视频按明确后的定义重新开始。两个阶段的误差数字不能直接比较。

第一阶段的结论（第 1–3 步）

前提：瞳孔上点标在看得见的、没有被眼睑挡住的瞳孔的最高处。

  1. batch size 用多少？用 2。按内部验证损失选出；在测试集上四种 batch size 相差不大（第 1 步）。
  2. 用哪个模型？用 ResNet-50（第 2 步）。
  3. 用哪种纠错算法挑要补标的帧？用 jump。三种算法没有明显差距，jump 略微领先（第 3 步）。

中间的逻辑：为什么换了标准还沿用 ResNet-50 和 batch 2

2026 年 9 月 20 日把 8 个点各自标在哪里明确了下来（逐点对照见第 4 步）。和之前相比有三处变化：瞳孔上点从“看得见的瞳孔的最高处”改为“瞳孔椭圆真实的最高点”，上移约 50 px；瞳孔左、右两点从“看得见的瞳孔最靠左、最靠右的位置”改为椭圆横轴的两个端点；两个眼角从阴影和反光的内侧改到外侧，各外移 40–50 px。瞳孔下点和上下眼睑点的定义没有变。图像、任务和训练方式都没有变。batch size 和模型的选择取决于图像和训练方式，不取决于点标在哪里，所以我们认为这两项选择不受影响，继续使用 ResNet-50 和 batch 2。这是一个判断，没有按明确后的定义重新比较过 batch size 和模型。第一阶段的三项选择本身是在同一套定义下做的：当时这个视频上的全部训练帧和测试帧都按同一种标法（核对见第 4 步），所以 batch size 之间、模型之间、纠错算法之间的比较是公平的。

第二阶段的结论（第 4–11 步）

前提：把瞳孔看成椭圆，上点标在椭圆真实的最高点；被眼睑挡住时，由标注的人按椭圆估计它的位置。

  1. 同一个视频标多少帧够？约 80 帧以内就饱和：同一个 5 分钟视频按 2026 年 9 月 20 日明确的点位定义重新标注后重做，20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 帧的误差是 12.9 / 13.0 / 12.0 / 13.1 / 12.3 px，没有随帧数下降；之后每个视频的平台点也都不超过 80 帧（第 5 步）。第一阶段在同一个视频上从 80 帧加到 300 帧，误差也没有下降（第 3 步）。
  2. 换一只新老鼠怎么办？要重新标，标 20 帧就到约 10 px。第二只老鼠（当时的模型只见过一只老鼠）：0 帧 208 px，标 20 帧到 10.4 px。第三只老鼠（模型已见过两只老鼠）：0 帧 26 px，标 20 帧到 9.2 px。第二次换老鼠时，不标任何帧的起点误差小得多（第 5 步）。
  3. 同一只老鼠之后的每个视频要标多少帧？标 0–40 帧就到平台，已完成的 9 个里有 1 个需要 80 帧（第 5 步）。
  4. 20 帧已经接近饱和，第一批能不能减到 5 帧或 10 帧？不能取代 20 帧：19 个视频里 5 帧够用的只有 5 个、10 帧 7 个，事先无法判断是哪些，而且结果取决于正好取到哪几帧。这里比的是第一批的大小（从已标的 20 帧里取 5 帧或 10 帧），不是每步 5 帧的流程（第 6 步）。
  5. 加入新视频会不会让旧视频变差？不会（第 7 步）。
  6. 模型给出的哪些点准，置信度能不能信？瞳孔上点最不准（误差中位数 10.2 px，其余 7 个点 3.5–6.0 px）；每个点都是置信度越低误差越大，但瞳孔上点即使置信度高也有 8.3 px（第 8 步）。
  7. 瞳孔面积怎么算？按 2026 年 9 月 20 日明确的定义，瞳孔四个点都是椭圆端点，在 19 个视频的 940 帧测试帧上，四点法的面积误差中位数是 5.4%，三点法是 8.4%；视频 4 和 8 上面积仍然不准（第 9 步）。
  8. 眨眼怎么判断？先定了定义：非睁眼 = 眼睛闭上或眼睑盖住部分瞳孔。目前只做静态按帧分析。判据：每只老鼠一个像素阈值，上下眼睑距离低于它判非睁眼，Pluto 147.5 px、Terra 150.5 px，在 327 帧单帧盲评上选出，两只几乎相同，准确率 Pluto 94%、Terra 89%；模型的眼睑距离在 949 张测试帧上和标注只差 2 px。主要发现：9 个视频（3、4、8–12、15、18）有 21–79% 的帧低于阈值，半掩是那些录像的常态；眨眼是其中的子集，眨眼的动态之后系统分析（第 10 步）。对照用的比值规则"睁开高度 ÷ 眼宽 < 0.347"在视频 0–13 的 239 帧上准确率 91%、在视频 14–18 的 88 帧上 93%。

Task: DeepLabCut finds 8 points on every frame of a mouse-eye video: pupil top, bottom, left, right; upper and lower eyelid; nasal and temporal eye corner. From these we get pupil size, eye opening and blinks, to be aligned with brain recordings later.

Main question: On a new video, how many frames must a person label before the model is good enough?

The work has two phases, separated by the day the keypoint definitions were made explicit (2026-09-20). Phase 1 used the earlier definitions; only three choices that do not depend on the definitions are kept from it: batch size, model and error-correction algorithm. All other conclusions come from phase 2, which starts again from the same 5-minute video under the explicit definitions. Error numbers of the two phases cannot be compared directly.

Conclusions of phase 1 (steps 1–3)

Premise: pupil top is placed at the highest point of the visible pupil, the part not hidden by the eyelid.

  1. Which batch size? 2, chosen by the internal validation loss; on the test set the four batch sizes differ little (step 1).
  2. Which model? ResNet-50 (step 2).
  3. Which error-correction algorithm picks the frames to label next? jump. The three algorithms show no clear gap; jump is slightly ahead (step 3).

The logic in between: why ResNet-50 and batch 2 were kept after the standard changed

On 2026-09-20 the placement of each of the 8 points was made explicit (point-by-point comparison in step 4). Three things changed: pupil top moved from "the highest point of the visible pupil" to "the true top of the pupil ellipse", about 50 px higher; pupil left and right moved from "the leftmost and rightmost visible points of the pupil" to the two ends of the horizontal axis of the ellipse; and the two corners moved from the inner side of the shadow and of the reflections to the outer side, 40–50 px outwards each. The definitions of pupil bottom and of the two eyelid points did not change. The images, the task and the training procedure did not change. The choice of batch size and model depends on the images and the training procedure, not on where the points are placed, so we consider both choices unaffected and keep ResNet-50 with batch 2. This is a judgment; batch size and model were not compared again under the explicit definitions. The three choices of phase 1 were themselves made under one set of definitions: all training and test frames of that video were labeled in the same way at the time (checked in step 4), so the comparisons between batch sizes, between models and between error-correction algorithms are fair.

Conclusions of phase 2 (steps 4–11)

Premise: the pupil is treated as an ellipse and pupil top is placed at the true top of that ellipse; where the eyelid hides it, the labeler estimates its position from the ellipse.

  1. How many labels are enough within one video? It saturates within about 80 labels: the same 5-minute video, relabeled under the keypoint definitions made explicit on 2026-09-20 and redone, gives 12.9 / 13.0 / 12.0 / 13.1 / 12.3 px at 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 labels, with no decrease; and no later video has a plateau point above 80 labels (step 5). In phase 1 the same video showed no decrease from 80 to 300 labels either (step 3).
  2. What about a new mouse? It needs its own labels, and 20 of them reach about 10 px. Second mouse (the model had seen one mouse): 208 px at 0 labels, 10.4 px at 20. Third mouse (the model had seen two mice): 26 px at 0 labels, 9.2 px at 20. The second time the mouse changed, the starting error without any labels was far smaller (step 5).
  3. How many labels does each later video of the same mouse need? 0–40 reach the plateau; 1 of the 9 finished ones needed 80 (step 5).
  4. 20 labels already come close to saturation; could the first batch be cut to 5 or 10? They cannot replace 20: 5 labels are enough on only 5 of the 19 videos and 10 labels on 7, one cannot tell in advance which, and the result depends on which frames happen to be taken. What is compared is the size of the first batch (5 or 10 taken out of the 20 already labeled), not a procedure of 5 labels per step (step 6).
  5. Do new videos make old ones worse? No (step 7).
  6. Which points are accurate, and can the confidence be trusted? Pupil top is the least accurate (median error 10.2 px; the other 7 points 3.5–6.0 px); for every point a lower confidence goes with a larger error, but pupil top has 8.3 px even at high confidence (step 8).
  7. How to compute the pupil area? Under the definitions made explicit on 2026-09-20 all four pupil points are ellipse endpoints, and on the 940 test frames of 19 videos the four-point area has a median error of 5.4% against 8.4% for the three-point rule; on videos 4 and 8 the area is still not accurate (step 9).
  8. How to detect blinks? A definition was fixed first: not open = the eye is closed or the lid covers part of the pupil. So far the analysis is static, frame by frame. Criterion: one pixel threshold per mouse, not open when the eyelid distance is below it, Pluto 147.5 px, Terra 150.5 px, chosen on 327 blind single frames and nearly equal for the two mice, 94% (Pluto) and 89% (Terra) accurate; the model's eyelid distance is within 2 px of the labels on 949 test frames. Main finding: in 9 videos (3, 4, 8–12, 15, 18) 21–79% of the frames are below the threshold — a half-covered eye is the resting state of those recordings; blinks are a subset; blink dynamics will be analysed systematically later (step 10). The comparison rule "eye opening ÷ eye width < 0.347" is 91% accurate on the 239 frames of videos 0–13 and 93% on the 88 frames of videos 14–18.

指标怎么算

每个视频留出一组人工标注的测试帧，不参与训练，也不参与任何选择。模型对每一张测试帧给出 8 个点的坐标，坐标是原始视频画面上的像素（928 × 736，不缩放）。模型给出的每个点都参与计算，不按置信度筛掉；人没有标的点（例如闭眼帧里的瞳孔点）不参与计算。

  1. 单个点的误差：第 i 帧第 k 个点，模型预测 (x^ik,y^ik)，人工标注 (xik,yik)，误差是两点之间的直线距离：eik=(x^ik−xik)2+(y^ik−yik)2
  2. 中位帧 RMSE（第 3 步起使用的主要指标）：先对每一帧算这一帧里各点误差的均方根，RMSEi=1Ki∑keik2其中 Ki 是这一帧里人标了的点数（最多 8）；再取所有测试帧 RMSEi 的中位数。
  3. 全部关键点合在一起的 RMSE（第 1、2 步使用）：把所有测试帧、所有点的误差放在一起算一次均方根，RMSE=1N∑i,keik2其中 N 是人标了的点的总数（100 帧 × 8 点，最多 800）。少数错得很远的点会把它拉高，所以后来改用中位帧 RMSE。
  4. 其他几个数：“第 90 百分位”是 RMSEi 的第 90 百分位数；“误差超过 50 px 的测试帧”是 RMSEi > 50 的帧所占比例；“置信度 ≥ 0.6 的点”是人标了的点里模型置信度不低于 0.6 的比例。

数字越小越好。作为参照：按现在的点位定义，眼睛宽度（两个眼角之间的距离，各视频测试帧人工标注的中位数）在 363–497 px 之间，所以 10 px 约为眼宽的 2–3%，50 px 约为 10–14%。计算脚本都在仓库里：第 1、2 步是 v1_old_pupil_top/batch-size-selection/scripts/evaluate_eye_test_set.py 和 model-selection/scripts/ 里的同名脚本，第 3 步是 active-learning-jump-selection/scripts/round_train_pipeline.py，第 5 步起是 v2_new_pupil_top_video_scaling/scripts/scale_step.py；每次打分的结果写在各 results/ 文件夹的 csv 表里。每一步用的视频、数据、模型和指标，写在那一步开头的“基本信息”里。

训练配置（第 1、2 步选定，两个阶段都使用）：ResNet-50，batch 2。每个模型都从 ImageNet 预训练权重开始重新训练，不接着我们自己之前的模型继续训。

How the metrics are computed

Each video has a set of human-labeled test frames that are never used for training or for any choice. For every test frame the model gives the coordinates of the 8 points, in pixels of the original video frame (928 × 736, no rescaling). Every predicted point enters the calculation, none is dropped for low confidence; points the person did not label (for example the pupil points of a closed-eye frame) are left out.

  1. Error of one point: for point k of frame i, with prediction (x^ik,y^ik) and human label (xik,yik), the error is the straight-line distance between the two: eik=(x^ik−xik)2+(y^ik−yik)2
  2. Median frame RMSE (the main metric from step 3 on): first the root mean square of the point errors within each frame, RMSEi=1Ki∑keik2where Ki is the number of labeled points in that frame (at most 8); then the median of RMSEi over all test frames.
  3. RMSE over all keypoints (used in steps 1 and 2): one root mean square over the errors of all points of all test frames, RMSE=1N∑i,keik2where N is the total number of labeled points (100 frames × 8 points, at most 800). A few points that are far off pull it up, which is why the median frame RMSE was used later.
  4. The other numbers: "90th percentile" is the 90th percentile of RMSEi; "test frames above 50 px" is the share of frames with RMSEi > 50; "points with confidence ≥ 0.6" is the share of labeled points whose model confidence is at least 0.6.

Lower is better. For scale: under the present keypoint definitions the eye width (distance between the two corners, median of the human labels on each video's test frames) is 363–497 px, so 10 px is about 2–3% of the eye width and 50 px about 10–14%. The scoring scripts are in the repository: v1_old_pupil_top/batch-size-selection/scripts/evaluate_eye_test_set.py and the script of the same name under model-selection/scripts/ for steps 1 and 2, active-learning-jump-selection/scripts/round_train_pipeline.py for step 3, and v2_new_pupil_top_video_scaling/scripts/scale_step.py from step 5 on; every score is written to the csv tables of the results/ folders. The video, data, model and metric of every step are listed in the "Basic information" table at its start.

Training recipe (chosen in steps 1 and 2, used in both phases): ResNet-50, batch 2. Every model is trained anew from ImageNet-pretrained weights; it never continues from one of our earlier models.

基本信息Basic information

做了什么：目的是选一种纠错算法：模型训练好之后，由算法在视频里找出“模型可能出错的帧”，人只改正这些帧，再重新训练。这种做法叫主动学习。比较了三种算法，看哪一种挑出的帧最能降低误差。做法：从 80 帧起步，每轮由算法挑帧，人工改正 20 帧加进去，重新训练。三种找帧算法各走一条线，都是 DeepLabCut 自带的（extract_outlier_frames），输入是模型对整段视频每一帧的预测：uncertain，8 个点里任何一个点的置信度低于 0.6 的帧；jump，任何一个点相对上一帧移动超过 20 px 的帧；fitting，对每个点的轨迹拟合一个时间序列模型（ARIMA，只用置信度高的帧拟合），8 个点偏离拟合位置的平均距离超过 20 px 的帧。找出的可疑帧再按画面外观聚成 20 类，每类取一帧给人改正。一共 11 轮，每条线从 80 帧加到 300 帧。

What was done:The aim is to choose an error-correction algorithm: once a model is trained, an algorithm searches the video for frames where the model is probably wrong, a person corrects only those frames, and the model is retrained. This procedure is called active learning. Three algorithms were compared to see whose picks reduce the error most. Procedure: starting from 80 labels, each round the algorithm picks frames, a person corrects 20 of them, they are added and the model is retrained. Three detectors ran as three branches, all built into DeepLabCut (extract_outlier_frames) and all working on the model's prediction of every frame of the video: uncertain, frames in which any of the 8 points has confidence below 0.6; jump, frames in which any point moved more than 20 px from the previous frame; fitting, frames in which the 8 points deviate on average by more than 20 px from a time-series model (ARIMA, fitted on the confident frames only) of each point's trajectory. The flagged frames are then clustered into 20 groups by image appearance and one frame per group is given to the labeler. 11 rounds, each branch from 80 to 300 labels.

结论：这一步有两个结论：纠错算法选 jump；在这个视频上，标注到约 80 帧就饱和。

  1. 纠错算法选 jump，之后的实验统一用它挑帧。在这次实验里 jump 的误差最低：11 轮的平均中位误差是 jump 16.4 px、uncertain 17.0 px、fitting 17.0 px，11 轮里有 8 轮 jump 最低。领先幅度约 0.6 px，小于同一设置重新训练一次时的波动（0.3–2 px），所以我们认为这几种纠错算法差距不大，jump 略微领先。
  2. 关于 jump 的阈值：这一步用的是固定的 20 px。从 2026 年 9 月 18 日起（第 5 步的主线），阈值改为该视频眼宽中位数的 3%，算法本身不变。原因是这一步的 5 分钟视频里眼睛约 700 px 宽，20 px 约为眼宽的 3%；后面的视频眼睛只有 360–500 px 宽，固定 20 px 会相当于眼宽的 4–6%，标准变严，所以改成相对值，使不同视频用同一个标准（在后面的视频上约 11–15 px）。这个改动记录在 v1_old_pupil_top/new-video-generalization/FROZEN_PARAMETERS.md。
  3. 在这个视频上，约 80 帧就饱和。从 80 帧加到 300 帧，三条线的中位误差一直在 15.7–17.5 px 之间，没有下降。
    • 这个结果用的是 2026 年 9 月 20 日之前的点位定义。
    • 2026 年 9 月 20 日明确点位定义之后，在同一个视频上重新标注、重做了一遍，结论一致：20、40、60、80、100 帧的误差都在 12–13 px 之间，没有随帧数下降（第 5 步，视频 0）。明确的内容见第 4 步，主要是瞳孔上点从“看得见的瞳孔的最高处”改为“瞳孔椭圆真实的最高点”，两个眼角明确为睑缘延长线的交点。
    • 两套定义下的误差数值不能直接比较，一致的是“约 80 帧以内就饱和”。

Conclusion:This step has two conclusions: jump is chosen as the error-correction algorithm, and on this video about 80 labels saturate.

  1. jump is the error-correction algorithm and picks the frames in all later experiments. In this experiment jump has the lowest error: the mean of the median error over the 11 rounds is 16.4 px for jump, 17.0 px for uncertain and 17.0 px for fitting, and jump is the lowest in 8 of the 11 rounds. The lead is about 0.6 px, smaller than the variation when one setting is trained again (0.3–2 px), so we regard the three error-correction algorithms as close, with jump slightly ahead.
  2. On the jump threshold: this step used a fixed 20 px. From 2026-09-18 on (the main line of step 5) the threshold is 3% of the video's median eye width; the algorithm is unchanged. Reason: in the 5-minute video of this step the eye is about 700 px wide, so 20 px is about 3% of the eye width; in the later videos the eye is only 360–500 px wide and a fixed 20 px would be 4–6% of it, a stricter criterion, so it was made relative to give every video the same standard (about 11–15 px on the later videos). The change is recorded in v1_old_pupil_top/new-video-generalization/FROZEN_PARAMETERS.md.
  3. On this video about 80 labels saturate. From 80 to 300 labels the median error of all three branches stays between 15.7 and 17.5 px, with no decrease.
    • This result uses the keypoint definitions from before 2026-09-20.
    • After the definitions were made explicit on 2026-09-20, the same video was relabeled and the experiment repeated, with the same conclusion: the error stays between 12 and 13 px at 20, 40, 60, 80 and 100 labels and does not fall with the number of labels (step 5, video 0). What was made explicit is described in step 4; mainly, pupil top moved from "the highest point of the visible pupil" to "the true top of the pupil ellipse", and the two corners became the crossing of the continued eyelid margins.
    • The error values under the two sets of definitions cannot be compared directly; what agrees is that the video saturates within about 80 labels.

What was done:Where each of the 8 points is placed was written down, and all earlier videos were relabeled accordingly. From this step on every model and number uses these definitions. The full definitions, example figures and the common mistakes are in the folder keypoint_definitions/ of the repository. The points are described one group at a time below, each followed by its figure.

  • 瞳孔上、下、左、右（图 4-2）：把瞳孔看作一个椭圆，四个点是椭圆两条轴的四个端点。被上眼睑或毛挡住的部分按椭圆补全，所以瞳孔上点常常落在上眼睑点的上方，也就是标在被眼睑盖住的位置上。图 4-2 里红点是现在的位置；黄色叉是 2026 年 9 月 20 日之前的位置：标在看得见的瞳孔最靠上、下、左、右的地方，没有按椭圆去拟合。瞳孔边界取变暗区域的外缘；边界出现两层轮廓时取左边那层（2026 年 9 月 26 日）；黑色核心外面较亮的一圈也算瞳孔（2026 年 9 月 29 日）。
  • Pupil top, bottom, left, right (Fig. 4-2): the pupil is treated as an ellipse and the four points are the ends of its two axes. Parts hidden by the upper eyelid or by fur are completed with the ellipse, so the pupil-top point often lies above the upper-eyelid point, that is, at a position covered by the lid. In Fig. 4-2 the red dots are the present positions; the yellow crosses are the positions before 2026-09-20: at the topmost, bottommost, leftmost and rightmost visible points of the pupil, without fitting an ellipse. The pupil boundary is the outer edge of the darkened region; where the edge looks doubled the left contour is used (2026-09-26); the brighter band around the dark core counts as pupil (2026-09-29).
  • 上眼睑、下眼睑（见上面的图 4-1）：眼皮和眼球的分界线叫睑缘。上眼睑点是上睑缘的中点，下眼睑点是下睑缘的中点。
  • Upper and lower eyelid (Fig. 4-1 above): the line between the eyelid skin and the eyeball is the eyelid margin. The upper-eyelid point is the midpoint of the upper margin, the lower-eyelid point the midpoint of the lower margin.
  • 左眼角（鼻侧，内眦；图 4-3 第二行）：眼角处有一片阴影，往下越来越窄，一直延伸到下眼睑附近。取阴影外侧的边作为左下方的睑缘（红色实线），把它和上睑缘各自延长（红色虚线），两条延长线的交点（红圈）就是左眼角，青色点是标注的位置。不取的画法（黄色点线和黄色叉）：沿着阴影内侧的边画，交点落在阴影里面。图中斜线区域就是两种画法之间的阴影。
  • 右眼角（颞侧，外眦；图 4-3 第三行）：右侧有几条反光亮带，亮带下面接着一段阴影。把下睑缘沿外层那条线向上延伸（红色虚线），绕到反光的右侧，与上睑缘相交的点（红圈）就是右眼角，青色点是标注的位置。取外侧的原因：2026 年 9 月 20 日和 Kaiwen 讨论后认为，皮毛不会反光，所以右侧这些反光亮带仍然在眼睛内部，眼角应该在反光的外面。不取的画法（黄色点线和黄色叉）：沿着反光的内沿和它下面阴影的内边缘画，交点落在紧挨反光的内层线上。也不标在反光亮带上。
  • Left corner (nasal, medial canthus; Fig. 4-3, second row): there is a shadow at this corner; it gets narrower downwards and continues to near the lower eyelid. The outer border of the shadow is taken as the lower-left margin (solid red); it and the upper margin are each continued (dashed red), and the crossing of the two continuations (red circle) is the left corner; the cyan dot is the labeled position. Not used (dotted yellow line and yellow cross): following the inner border of the shadow, which puts the crossing inside the shadow. The hatched area is the shadow between the two constructions.
  • Right corner (temporal, lateral canthus; Fig. 4-3, third row): there are several bright reflections on this side, with a stretch of shadow below them. The lower margin is continued upwards along the outer line (dashed red), around the right of the reflections, and the point where it meets the upper margin (red circle) is the right corner; the cyan dot is the labeled position. Why the outer side: in the discussion with Kaiwen on 2026-09-20 we concluded that fur does not reflect, so the bright reflections on the right are still inside the eye and the corner must lie outside them. Not used (dotted yellow line and yellow cross): following the inner edge of the reflection and of the shadow below it, which puts the crossing on the inner line next to the reflection. The corner is not placed on a reflection either.
  • 看不清的点：所有 8 个点都一样，看得清楚就标；不能准确判断位置时，这个点留空不标。
  • Points that cannot be seen: the same for all 8 points: a point is labeled when it can be seen clearly and left empty when its position cannot be judged accurately.

5.1 每一批 20 帧是怎么挑出来的（算法）5.1 How each batch of 20 frames is picked (algorithm)

脚本：v2_new_pupil_top_video_scaling/scripts/select_frames.py。下面的数字都是脚本里固定的参数，照着做可以得到同样的帧。

（一）先把视频分成三段

  1. 测试段：视频最后 10%（不少于 60 秒）。在这一段里按时间等间隔取 50 帧，作为测试帧。
  2. 验证段：测试段之前的 10%（不少于 60 秒）。等间隔取 20 帧，作为验证帧。
  3. 候选池：验证段之前的全部帧（约前 80%；19.9 分钟的视频是 57158 帧）。训练用的帧只从这里挑。验证段两侧各去掉 2 秒，避免三段之间有几乎相同的相邻帧。

（二）第 1 批：按画面外观聚类（不用任何模型）

  1. 在候选池里每 5 帧取 1 帧。
  2. 给每一帧算一个“外观指纹”：转成灰度图，缩小到 128 × 96；用标准差 1 px 的高斯模糊图减去标准差 6 px 的高斯模糊图（留下边缘和形状，去掉缓慢的明暗变化）；再缩小到 32 × 24，展开成 768 个数；减去均值、除以标准差。
  3. 对这些指纹做 k-means 聚类，分成 20 类（scikit-learn 的 KMeans，n_clusters=20, n_init=10, max_iter=300, random_state=42）。
  4. 每一类取离类中心最近的那一帧（欧氏距离），共 20 帧。从帧数最多的类开始取。
  5. 间隔要求：取的帧之间、以及和已经标过的帧之间，至少相隔 1 秒；不满足的跳过。如果因此不足 20 帧，把类数增加到 30、40、…（最多 80）重新聚类，用新的类中心帧补足。

（三）第 2 批起：先用 jump 规则找候选帧，再按外观聚类

  1. 用上一步训练出的模型预测整段视频的每一帧。
  2. 算阈值：眼宽 = 每一帧鼻侧眼角到颞侧眼角的距离，取整段视频的中位数；阈值 = 眼宽中位数 × 3%（约 11–15 px）。
  3. 算跳动：对每一帧，8 个点各自相对上一帧移动的距离，取其中最大的一个。
  4. 候选池里跳动超过阈值的帧就是候选帧（被 jump 标出的帧）。候选帧超过 12000 帧时等间隔抽稀到约 12000 帧；少于 40 帧时改用跳动最大的 2000 帧。
  5. 对候选帧做和第 1 批完全相同的步骤：算外观指纹、k-means 分 20 类、每类取离中心最近的一帧、相隔至少 1 秒、不足时增加类数。
  6. 如果还不足 20 帧（模型已经很好、候选帧很少且挤在几秒内时会发生），按跳动从大到小从候选帧里补，仍要求相隔 1 秒。

所以每一批都是“一类一帧”：jump 规则决定去哪里找（模型不稳的帧），聚类保证挑出的 20 帧画面各不相同，而不是 20 张几乎一样的帧。

Script: v2_new_pupil_top_video_scaling/scripts/select_frames.py. All numbers below are fixed parameters of the script; following them gives the same frames.

(a) The video is first split into three segments

  1. Test segment: the final 10% of the video (at least 60 s). 50 frames evenly spaced in time are the test frames.
  2. Validation segment: the 10% before it (at least 60 s). 20 evenly spaced frames are the validation frames.
  3. Pool: all frames before the validation segment (about the first 80%; 57158 frames for a 19.9-min video). Training frames are picked from the pool only. 2 s are removed on both sides of the validation segment so that the three segments share no near-identical neighbouring frames.

(b) Batch 1: clustering by image appearance (no model)

  1. Every 5th frame of the pool is taken.
  2. An "appearance fingerprint" is computed for each frame: greyscale, downscaled to 128 × 96; a Gaussian blur with sigma 1 px minus a Gaussian blur with sigma 6 px (keeps edges and shape, removes slow lighting changes); downscaled to 32 × 24 and flattened to 768 numbers; mean subtracted and divided by the standard deviation.
  3. k-means on the fingerprints with 20 clusters (scikit-learn KMeans, n_clusters=20, n_init=10, max_iter=300, random_state=42).
  4. From each cluster the frame closest to the cluster centre (Euclidean distance) is taken, 20 frames in total, starting with the largest cluster.
  5. Spacing: picked frames must be at least 1 s apart from each other and from every frame labeled earlier; frames that violate this are skipped. If fewer than 20 remain, the number of clusters is raised to 30, 40, … (at most 80) and the new centre frames fill the batch.

(c) From batch 2 on: candidates by the jump rule, then clustering by appearance

  1. The model of the previous step predicts every frame of the whole video.
  2. Threshold: eye width = distance from the nasal to the temporal corner in each frame, median over the video; threshold = 3% of the median eye width (about 11–15 px).
  3. Jump: for each frame, the distance each of the 8 points moved from the previous frame; the largest of the 8.
  4. Pool frames whose jump exceeds the threshold are the candidates (the frames flagged by the jump rule). More than 12000 candidates are thinned evenly to about 12000; with fewer than 40, the 2000 frames with the largest jumps are used instead.
  5. The candidates go through exactly the steps of batch 1: fingerprints, k-means with 20 clusters, the frame closest to each centre, at least 1 s apart, more clusters if short.
  6. If there are still fewer than 20 (this happens when the model is already good and the few candidates are bunched within a few seconds), the batch is filled from the candidates in order of decreasing jump, still 1 s apart.

Every batch is therefore "one frame per cluster": the jump rule decides where to look (frames on which the model is unstable), and the clustering makes sure the 20 picked frames look different from each other instead of being 20 nearly identical frames.

做了什么：从每个视频已经标好的第一批 20 帧里取 5 帧或 10 帧，其余条件和该视频第 1 步完全相同，重新训练并打分，不需要新的标注。帧数这么少时，结果很受“正好取到哪几帧”的影响，为了避免偶然性，5 帧和 10 帧各取两组不同的帧，分别训练，记为 5a、5b 和 10a、10b；图上的色带就是两组结果之间的范围。

  1. 第一批的 20 帧来自 20 个外观类（第 5 步 5.1 节的第 1 批算法），每一类有一个类中心（768 个数的外观指纹）和这一类的帧数。

How exactly the 5 and 10 labels are taken (algorithm). Script: v2_new_pupil_top_video_scaling/scripts/make_label_subsets.py. It uses only the 20 labeled frames of the first batch and the clustering saved at the time; no model and no new labeling.

  1. The 20 frames of the first batch come from 20 appearance clusters (batch-1 algorithm of section 5.1 in step 5); each cluster has a centre (a 768-number fingerprint) and a number of frames.
  2. The 20 cluster centres are clustered again by k-means into 5 groups (for 5 labels) or 10 groups (for 10 labels), each centre weighted by the number of frames in its cluster (scikit-learn KMeans, n_clusters=5 or 10, n_init=20, random_state=0).
  3. Each of the 20 labeled frames is assigned by its fingerprint to the nearest group.
  4. Subset a: from each group, the labeled frame closest to the group centre. Subset b: from each group, the second closest (the same frame again when the group holds only one labeled frame).
  5. If groups returned the same frame and fewer than 5 or 10 distinct frames result, the subset is filled from the unused labeled frames in order of increasing distance to any group centre.
  6. This gives four lists: 5a, 5b, 10a, 10b. In training, the video's own 20 labels are replaced by the 5 or 10 listed frames; the carried labels of the earlier videos, the validation frames (20), the test frames (50), the model and the training settings are exactly those of that video's step 1 (scale_step.py --step 1 --subset list-file).

So the 5 and 10 labels are also "one frame per kind of appearance", only with a coarser grouping; a and b are two different sets of frames under the same grouping (the closest and the second closest frame of each group) In the 19 videos 5a and 5b have no frame in common (except 1 frame each on videos 12 and 14), and 10a and 10b share 2–5 frames.

8.1 模型训练饱和后，如何有效选出不可信的帧：比较三种纠错算法的适用场景8.1 After training has saturated, how to pick out unreliable frames: the three outlier algorithms by use case

目的。第 3 步比较了三种纠错算法（jump、uncertain、fitting）哪一种最适合在主动学习里挑下一批要标注的帧，选了 jump。这里的问题不同：模型训练饱和、对整段视频预测完之后，哪些帧的预测不可信。所以把同样三种算法再比一次：量每种算法标出的帧里有多少真的不可信、真的不可信的帧里有多少被标出，再按三种用途（人工复核、挑训练帧、剔除不可信帧）分别看哪一种合适。

用的帧。每个视频（视频 0–18）的 50 帧测试帧和 20 帧验证帧，共 19 × 70 = 1330 帧。这些帧按时间等间隔取，不是任何算法挑的；每帧都有人工标注；模型没有用它们训练。

真值和两档不可信。每帧用该视频平台点的模型算帧 RMSE：预测的 8 个点和标注的 8 个点之间的距离（公式见页面开头）。帧 RMSE 越大，这一帧的预测离标注越远。按它分两档：非常不可信 = 帧 RMSE 超过 50 px（约眼宽的 10–14%，预测点离标注位置很远），1330 帧里有 32 帧（2.5%）；轻微不可信 = 超过 20 px（约眼宽的 4–6%，预测点在标注位置附近但有偏差），有 155 帧（11.7%，含上面的 32 帧）。50 px 和 20 px 这两个界限是我们定的，没有用数据选。

三种算法：每种算什么、阈值多少、阈值从哪来。每种算法对每一帧算出一个数，只用模型的预测，不用标注；这个数越过它的阈值，就把这一帧标为不可信。

  • jump。算什么：8 个点里相对上一帧移动最远的那个点的位移，除以该视频眼宽的中位数。阈值：0.03，即眼宽的 3%，在各视频上约 11–15 px。来源：DLC 自带的 jump 用固定的 20 px，第 3 步用的就是它；第 5 步起主线把它换算成眼宽的 3%（视频 0 的眼宽约 700 px，20 px 约为 3%），使眼宽不同的视频用同一个相对标准；这里用的就是第 5 步以来主线用的这条规则。
  • uncertain。算什么：8 个点里最低的置信度。阈值：0.6。来源：DLC 配置里 pcutoff 的默认值，DLC 画图和过滤时把置信度低于它的点当不可信。DLC 自带的 uncertain 算法用的是 0.01；在这 1330 帧上，0.01 一帧都标不出来，所以没有用。
  • fitting。算什么：对每个点的 x 坐标和 y 坐标，各自在整段视频上拟合 SARIMAX(3,0,1) 时间序列模型（置信度低于 0.01 的点当缺失，序列先减去均值）；一个点的偏差 = 模型预测的位置和时间序列一步预测的位置之间的距离；fitting = 8 个点偏差的平均。阈值：20 px。来源：DLC 的默认值，没有改。算法按 DLC 文档用 statsmodels 重新实现，因为 DLC 的函数只输出整段视频的标记，不输出每帧的偏差。

另外两条规则。（4）uncertain 平均：8 个点置信度的平均值低于 t。t 不是默认值，是在这 1330 帧上选的：把 19 个视频中的一个拿出来，用其余 18 个视频的 1260 帧选出让轻微不可信帧（超过 20 px）的检出率减误报率最大的 t，再用这个 t 判定拿出来的那个视频的 70 帧；19 个视频轮流拿出一次，所以每一帧都是用没有见过它所在视频的 t 判定的。在全部 1330 帧上选出的 t 是 0.79。（5）jump 或 uncertain：两条里任一条标出就标出。图和表里五条规则的名字：jump、uncertain（最低置信度 < 0.6）、uncertain 平均（平均置信度 < t）、jump 或 uncertain、fitting。

怎么评。每条规则报两个数：准确率 = 标出的帧里真的不可信的比例；检出率 = 真的不可信的帧里被标出的比例。另外对每种算法算 AUC：把阈值从最小值到最大值逐个试，每个阈值得到一对（误报率，检出率），连成曲线，AUC 是曲线下的面积；1 表示所有不可信帧的数都比可信帧更极端，0.5 表示和随机挑一样。AUC 不依赖阈值。

脚本和结果。scripts/unreliable_frames.py；results/unreliable_frames/（每帧一行的 unreliable_frames_all_videos.csv，每条规则一行的 unreliable_rules_all_videos.csv，每个视频一行的 unreliable_signals_all_videos.csv）。

Purpose. Step 3 compared the three outlier algorithms (jump, uncertain, fitting) as pickers of the next frames to label in active learning and chose jump. The question here is different: once training has saturated and the model has predicted a whole video, which frames are unreliable. So the same three algorithms are compared again: for each, the share of flagged frames that are truly unreliable and the share of truly unreliable frames that are flagged, and then, for three uses (human review, picking training frames, removing unreliable frames), which algorithm suits.

Frames used. The 50 test frames and 20 validation frames of every video (videos 0–18), 19 × 70 = 1330 frames. They are evenly spaced in time, not picked by any algorithm; every frame is human-labeled; no model was trained on them.

Truth and the two levels. For each frame the frame RMSE of the video's plateau-point model: the distance between the 8 predicted and the 8 labeled points (formula at the top of the page). The larger it is, the farther the prediction is from the labels. Two levels: very unreliable = frame RMSE above 50 px (about 10–14% of the eye width; predicted points far from the labeled positions), 32 of the 1330 frames (2.5%); mildly unreliable = above 20 px (about 4–6% of the eye width; predicted points near the labeled positions but offset), 155 frames (11.7%, including the 32). The limits 50 px and 20 px are ours and were not chosen from data.

The three algorithms: what each computes, its threshold, where the threshold comes from. Each algorithm gives every frame one number from the model's predictions alone, without labels; the frame is flagged as unreliable when that number crosses the threshold.

  • jump. Computes: the displacement of the keypoint that moved farthest from the previous frame, divided by the video's median eye width. Threshold: 0.03, i.e. 3% of the eye width, about 11–15 px per video. Origin: DLC's own jump uses a fixed 20 px, which step 3 used; from step 5 the main line converted it to 3% of the eye width (video 0 has an eye about 700 px wide, where 20 px is about 3%) so that videos with different eye widths share one relative standard; the rule used here is the main line's rule since step 5.
  • uncertain. Computes: the lowest confidence of the 8 points. Threshold: 0.6. Origin: the default pcutoff of the DLC config, below which DLC treats a point as unreliable when plotting and filtering. DLC's own uncertain algorithm uses 0.01; on these 1330 frames 0.01 flags no frame at all, so it was not used.
  • fitting. Computes: for each keypoint's x and y coordinate a SARIMAX(3,0,1) time-series model fitted to the whole video (points with confidence below 0.01 treated as missing, mean subtracted first); deviation of a keypoint = distance between the model's predicted position and the one-step prediction of the time series; fitting = mean deviation over the 8 keypoints. Threshold: 20 px. Origin: DLC's default, unchanged. The algorithm is re-implemented with statsmodels after DLC's documentation, because DLC's function only outputs the flags for a whole video, not the per-frame deviation.

Two further rules. (4) uncertain mean: the mean confidence of the 8 points below t. t is not a default; it was chosen on these 1330 frames: one of the 19 videos is set aside, on the 1260 frames of the other 18 the t that maximizes recall minus false-alarm rate for the mildly unreliable frames (above 20 px) is chosen, and that t judges the 70 frames of the set-aside video; each of the 19 videos is set aside once, so every frame is judged with a t chosen without its own video. On all 1330 frames the chosen t is 0.79. (5) jump or uncertain: flagged when either of the two flags. The five rules are named in the figure and table as: jump, uncertain (lowest confidence < 0.6), uncertain mean (mean confidence < t), jump or uncertain, fitting.

How they are scored. Two numbers per rule: precision = share of the flagged frames that are truly unreliable; recall = share of the truly unreliable frames that are flagged. In addition an AUC per algorithm: the threshold is moved from the smallest to the largest value, each value gives one pair (false-alarm rate, recall), the pairs form a curve, and the AUC is the area under it; 1 means every unreliable frame has a more extreme value than every reliable frame, 0.5 means no better than random picking. The AUC does not depend on the threshold.

判据（按帧）。（1）眼睑点正常且 O < T → 非睁眼，T 每只老鼠一个值：Pluto 147.5 px，Terra 150.5 px（图中标为 148 / 150）；mouse A 定不了（它的 12 帧盲评全是睁开）。（2）眼睑点失效的帧：四个瞳孔点的平均置信度 < 0.527 → 非睁眼，否则睁开。0.527 是在 2371 帧人工标注过、模型没训练过的帧上选出的值（43 帧标注者把四个瞳孔点全部留空，2328 帧四个点都标了，取让检出率减误报率最大的阈值；scripts/blink_and_area.py B 部分）。

阈值怎么选（图 10-1）。每只老鼠只用自己的盲评帧。候选阈值 T 从 60 px 到 300 px 每 1 px 试一次。每个 T 把帧分成四格：抓对 = 真值非睁眼且 O < T；漏掉 = 真值非睁眼且 O ≥ T；误报 = 真值睁开且 O < T；对 = 真值睁开且 O ≥ T。检出率 = 抓对 ÷ 真值非睁眼的帧数；误报率 = 误报 ÷ 真值睁开的帧数；一致率（准确率）= (抓对 + 对) ÷ 全部帧数。图里橙线是 检出率 − 误报率，蓝线是一致率；选的是橙线最高的 T，并列最高的一段取中点。用橙线不用蓝线，是因为蓝线把两类帧混在一起算，哪类多就偏向哪类（极端情况：若 90% 的帧睁开，阈值定到 0、一帧都不标，一致率也有 90%），橙线两类各算一个比例再相减，不受两类比例影响；这次两类帧数接近（Pluto 77 对 74，Terra 64 对 62），两条线的顶点落在同一个 T。代入实际数字：Terra 126 帧（非睁眼 64、睁开 62），T = 150.5 时抓对 59、漏掉 5、误报 9、对 53，检出率 59 ÷ 64 = 92.2%，误报率 9 ÷ 62 = 14.5%，橙线 77.7，一致率 (59 + 53) ÷ 126 = 88.9%；Pluto 151 帧（77、74），T = 147.5 时 71、6、3、71，检出率 92.2%，误报率 4.1%，橙线 88.2，一致率 94.0%。另外做留一视频检验：轮流拿掉一个视频的帧选 T，看 T 的范围。

前提核对：眼睛位置是否固定。以两个眼角的中点为眼睛位置，Pluto 的 7 个较晚的日子（10-27 到 10-31）在 x 方向相差 10 px、y 方向 21 px 以内，Terra 的 8 天相差 35 / 24 px 以内，视频内部的移动也只有 ±10–15 px，相对 120–180 px 的睁开高度算稳定。例外是 Pluto 最早的三天（10-22、10-23、10-24）：眼睛位置向右下偏了 45–60 px，眼宽也最小（392–428 px，其他日子 417–468 px），睁开高度中位数也最小（117–156 px）。10 月 13 日只用标注者自己标的眼角点复核（不经过模型）：10-23 的 80 帧、10-22 的 40 帧里，眼睛中心比 10-28 到 10-31 的 300 帧向右 42 / 59 px、向下 17 / 34 px，10-24 偏 13 / 7 px，偏移是真的。但上下眼睑的像素距离只看两点之差，整体平移不改变它，所以位置偏移本身不影响像素阈值；剩下的疑点只有这两天眼宽小 5–10%（相机略远也会这样），而这两天也是眯得最多的两天，眼宽变小和眯眼分不开，录制时每天核对老鼠的位置，因此按同一阈值处理。

Definition. Each frame is in one of two states: open, or not open (the eye is closed, or the eyelid covers part of the pupil). Blink / long closure / squint come with the systematic analysis of the dynamics later.

Why a pixel distance and one value per mouse. The question is when the eye is narrowed. The camera is fixed and the mouse is placed at the same position every day (checked below), so the pixel distance between the two eyelid points is itself comparable across days and needs no division by the eye width; eye size differs between mice, so each mouse gets its own threshold. For the candidates 120 / 140 / 160 / 180 px the closest example frames were drawn so that the eye opening at each value can be checked by eye (Figures 10-2, 10-3).

Criterion (per frame). (1) Eyelid points plausible and O < T → not open, with one T per mouse: Pluto 147.5 px, Terra 150.5 px (shown as 148 / 150 in the figures); mouse A cannot be set (all 12 of its judged frames are open). (2) Frames with implausible eyelid points: not open if the mean confidence of the four pupil points is < 0.527, otherwise open. 0.527 was chosen on 2371 human-labeled frames the model was not trained on (43 in which the labeler left all four pupil points empty, 2328 with all four labeled; the threshold maximizing recall − false-alarm rate; part B of scripts/blink_and_area.py).

How the threshold is chosen (Figure 10-1). Each mouse uses only its own judged frames. Candidate thresholds T run from 60 to 300 px in steps of 1 px. Each T splits the frames into four cells: hit = truth not open and O < T; miss = truth not open and O ≥ T; false alarm = truth open and O < T; correct rejection = truth open and O ≥ T. Recall = hits ÷ frames whose truth is not open; false-alarm rate = false alarms ÷ frames whose truth is open; agreement (accuracy) = (hits + correct rejections) ÷ all frames. The orange line is recall − false-alarm rate, the blue line the agreement; the T with the highest orange value is chosen, the middle of a tied range. Orange rather than blue because the blue line pools the two classes and leans toward whichever is more frequent (in the extreme, with 90% of the frames open, a threshold of 0 that flags nothing still agrees 90%), whereas orange takes one rate per class and subtracts, so it does not depend on the class proportions; here the classes are nearly balanced (Pluto 77 against 74, Terra 64 against 62) and both lines peak at the same T. With the actual numbers: Terra, 126 frames (64 not open, 62 open), at T = 150.5 hits 59, misses 5, false alarms 9, correct rejections 53, recall 59 ÷ 64 = 92.2%, false-alarm rate 9 ÷ 62 = 14.5%, orange 77.7, agreement (59 + 53) ÷ 126 = 88.9%; Pluto, 151 frames (77, 74), at T = 147.5 the cells are 71, 6, 3, 71, recall 92.2%, false-alarm rate 4.1%, orange 88.2, agreement 94.0%. A leave-one-video-out check repeats the choice with each video's frames removed in turn and reports the range of T.

Premise check: is the eye position fixed. With the midpoint of the two eye corners as the eye position, Pluto's seven later days (10-27 to 10-31) lie within 10 px in x and 21 px in y of each other, Terra's eight days within 35 / 24 px, and the movement inside a video is only ±10–15 px, which is stable against openings of 120–180 px. The exception is Pluto's three earliest days (10-22, 10-23, 10-24): the eye sits 45–60 px to the lower right, the eye width is the smallest (392–428 px against 417–468 px on the other days) and so is the median opening (117–156 px). On 2026-10-13 this was checked with the labeler's own corner labels only (no model): in the 80 frames of 10-23 and the 40 of 10-22 the eye centre is 42 / 59 px to the right and 17 / 34 px lower than in the 300 frames of 10-28 to 10-31, 10-24 is off by 13 / 7 px, so the shift is real. But the eyelid distance in pixels is a difference of two points and a translation leaves it unchanged, so the shift itself does not affect the pixel threshold; what remains is that the eye width is 5–10% smaller on those two days (a slightly more distant camera would do that too), and they are also the two most narrowed days, so a smaller width cannot be told from narrowing. The mouse position is checked at every recording, so the same threshold is used.

结论：按像素定，两只老鼠的阈值几乎相同：Pluto 147.5 px、Terra 150.5 px，一个 150 px 左右的值两只都能用。
  1. 整条逻辑：①定义：非睁眼 = 闭上或眼睑盖住部分瞳孔。②真值：标注者按这个定义盲判 327 个单帧，分层抽样让边界帧够多。③量：模型给每帧的上下眼睑像素距离 O。④选阈值：每只老鼠在自己的帧上扫 60–300 px，取"检出率 − 误报率"最高的 T（图 10-1），留一视频检验稳定。⑤验证一：判过的帧放回全部帧的分布上，两类分得开，错的只在线旁 20 px 内（图 10-5）。⑥验证二：用标注者自己标的 949 张测试帧量模型在 O 上的误差，中位 2 px、无偏差，只翻 2–4% 的帧（图 10-6）。所以阈值附近剩下的错，大部分不是模型的，是边界帧本身模糊、以及单一个像素数抓不住标注者判断时看的别的东西（瞳孔露出多少、眼睑形状）。⑦没有验证的：同一个人重判的一致性。
  2. 阈值的稳定性：留一视频的范围 Pluto 147.5–160.5 px、Terra 143.5–154.5 px。Pluto 的最优很平（147–160 px 准确率都在 93–94%），Terra 的更尖，准确率 88.9% 也低于 Pluto 的 94.0%，因为视频 11、18 这两个半掩的日子里很多帧正好骑在阈值附近。
  3. 每天的情况：睁得清楚的日子（视频 0、5、6、7、13、16、17）低于阈值的帧不到 1.1%；视频 1、2、14 为 3–14%；视频 3、4、8、9、10、11、12、15、18 有 21–79% 的帧低于阈值，其中 Pluto 的 10-22、10-23、10-24 和 Terra 的 10-17（视频 11）、10-08（视频 18）是 60–79%，整天眯着。非睁眼在这些录像里是一种状态，不是偶发事件；眨眼（短暂、完全闭合）只是它的一个子集，之后系统分析动态时需要在这个状态判断上再按深度和时长切分。
  4. 限制：（a）真值是一个人判的，重复性没量过。（b）样本按模型的睁开程度分层抽，边界档占比高于真实比例，所以表里的准确率是分层样本上的，不等于整段视频按帧的准确率。（c）阈值在这些帧上选、也在这些帧上打分，只有留一视频一道防线。（d）睁开高度是模型输出，但在 949 张测试帧上量过：模型的眼睑距离和标注差中位 2 px、无偏差，只把 2–4% 的帧翻到阈值另一边（图 10-6），所以阈值附近的错大部分不是跟踪错；例子图里仍有一帧眼睑点落在毛上。（e）像素阈值假设相机尺度不变。Pluto 最早两天眼睛位置偏了 42–59 px，平移不影响像素距离；这两天眼宽小 5–10%，和眯眼分不开，按同一阈值处理，这三天按像素有 45–79% 的帧低于阈值，按比值规则是 46–70%，方向相同。（f）视频 18 抽到的 17 帧标注者全判非睁眼，所以 Terra 的阈值主要由"哪些天整天眯着"决定，不是同一天内睁、眯的分界。（g）mouse A 定不了。（h）例子图只排 6 列，Terra 的 17、18 没进去；330 帧抽出的盲评里 3 帧未判。
Conclusion:In pixels the two mice come out almost the same: Pluto 147.5 px, Terra 150.5 px; a value of about 150 px would serve both.
  1. The whole chain: ① definition: not open = closed or the lid covers part of the pupil. ② truth: the labeler judged 327 single frames blind under that definition, stratified so that borderline frames are plentiful. ③ quantity: the model's eyelid distance O in pixels for every frame. ④ threshold: per mouse, on its own frames, scan 60–300 px and take the T maximizing recall − false-alarm rate (Figure 10-1), checked by leave-one-video-out. ⑤ check one: the judged frames put back on the distribution of all frames separate cleanly, errors only within 20 px of the line (Figure 10-5). ⑥ check two: the labeler's own 949 test-frame labels measure the model's error on O, median 2 px, no bias, flipping only 2–4% of the frames (Figure 10-6). So most of the remaining errors near the threshold are not the model's: borderline frames are ambiguous in themselves, and a single pixel number cannot capture what the labeler also looks at (how much pupil shows, the lid shape). ⑦ not checked: the same judge's consistency on a second pass.
  2. Stability of the thresholds: the leave-one-video-out ranges are Pluto 147.5–160.5 px and Terra 143.5–154.5 px. Pluto's optimum is flat (93–94% from 147 to 160 px), Terra's sharper, and its accuracy (88.9%) is lower than Pluto's (94.0%) because on the two half-covered days (videos 11, 18) many frames sit right at the threshold.
  3. Day by day: on the clearly open days (videos 0, 5, 6, 7, 13, 16, 17) under 1.1% of the frames fall below the threshold; videos 1, 2 and 14 have 3–14%; videos 3, 4, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 15 and 18 have 21–79% of their frames below it, among them Pluto's 10-22, 10-23, 10-24 and Terra's 10-17 (video 11) and 10-08 (video 18) with 60–79%, a narrowed eye all day. Not-open is a state in these recordings, not an occasional event; a blink (brief, full closure) is only a subset of it, and the systematic analysis of the dynamics will have to split this state further by depth and duration.
  4. Limits: (a) one judge, repeatability not measured. (b) The sample is stratified by the model's opening, with the borderline strata over-represented, so the accuracies are those of the stratified sample, not per-frame accuracies of whole videos. (c) The threshold is chosen and scored on the same frames; leave-one-video-out is the only guard. (d) The opening is model output, but it was measured on 949 test frames: the model's eyelid distance differs from the labels by 2 px median with no bias and flips only 2–4% of the frames across the threshold (Figure 10-6), so most errors near the threshold are not tracking errors; one example tile still has the eyelid points on fur. (e) A pixel threshold assumes a fixed camera scale. On Pluto's two earliest days the eye sits 42–59 px off, which a pixel distance ignores; their eye width is 5–10% smaller, indistinguishable from narrowing, so the same threshold is used; on those three days 45–79% of the frames are below the pixel threshold against 46–70% with the ratio rule, same direction. (f) All 17 judged frames of video 18 are not open, so Terra's threshold is driven mainly by which days are narrowed all day, not by an open / narrowed boundary within a day. (g) Mouse A cannot be set. (h) The example sheet has six columns, so Terra's videos 17 and 18 are missing; 3 of the 330 drawn frames were left unjudged.

12还没有结论的地方Where there is no conclusion yet

  • 平台规则在噪声边缘：测试集只有 50 帧，同一设置重复训练，中位误差本身会有约 0.5–1 px 的波动，而 3% 约等于 0.3 px。所以每个视频的平台点有大约 ±20 帧的不确定性。每个点只训练了一次。
  • 怎么找出预测不可信的帧（第 8 步 8.1 节）：fitting 检测器找明显错误最准（标 2% 的帧，找到 72%），jump 规则找到三分之二，置信度能找全但要标出一半的帧；还没有既全又准的规则，各视频之间差别也大；几种信号合用还没有试。
  • 视频 4、5、8 误差偏大（视频 8 一直在 25 px 以上）。目前的解释是标注的人的判断：画面比较模糊，肉眼也难以识别点位。这一点还没有用数字验证过：用整幅画面的清晰度（拉普拉斯方差）和亮度去量，这三个视频和其他 Pluto 视频没有差别，所以模糊如果存在，是在瞳孔边缘这样的局部，需要专门去量。
  • 第三只老鼠（Terra，视频 11–18）：8 个视频全部完成，平台点 20–60 帧。“换一只新老鼠要标 20 帧”目前只在两次换老鼠上各看到一次（视频 1 和视频 11）。
  • 面积曲线里的跳变（第 11 步）：眼睛睁开时某个瞳孔点在两个位置之间来回跳，闭眼规则标不出来；还没有针对它的规则，也还没有逐个事件对照原视频区分眨眼、眼球转动和跟踪错误。
  • 面积：正式的面积脚本目前仍是三点法，是否换成四点法，待定。眨眼：静态按帧分析按"非睁眼 = 闭上或部分盖住"的定义有了判据和验证（第 10 步）：每只老鼠一个像素阈值（Pluto 147.5、Terra 150.5 px，图 10-1 的选法），盲评准确率 94% / 89%，并在 949 张测试帧上验证了模型的眼睑距离误差只有 2 px（图 10-5、10-6）；还没有的是眨眼本身的定义和把眨眼从长时间半掩状态里分出来的规则，Kaiwen 给的方向是"眼睑高度先降后升算一次，更严格的版本要求有完全闭上的瞬间"。
  • The plateau rule works at the noise floor: With 50 test frames, retraining the same setting moves the median by about 0.5–1 px, while 3% is about 0.3 px. Each video's plateau point therefore carries an uncertainty of roughly ±20 labels. Every point is a single training run.
  • How to find unreliable frames (step 8, section 8.1): the fitting detector is the most precise for gross errors (flags 2% of the frames, finds 72%), the jump rule finds two thirds, the confidence finds all of them but flags half of the frames; there is no rule yet that is both complete and selective, videos differ a lot, and combinations of the signals have not been tried.
  • Videos 4, 5 and 8 have larger errors (video 8 stays above 25 px). The present explanation is the labeler's judgement: the image is blurry and the points are hard to identify even by eye. This has not been confirmed by a number: whole-image sharpness (variance of the Laplacian) and brightness do not differ between these three and the other Pluto videos, so any blur is local (e.g. at the pupil edge) and would need a dedicated measurement.
  • The third mouse (Terra, videos 11–18): all 8 videos are finished, plateau points at 20–60 labels. "A new mouse needs 20 labels" has so far been seen once at each of the two changes of mouse (videos 1 and 11).
  • Jumps in the area traces (step 11): with the eye open, one pupil point alternates between two positions and the closure rule does not flag it; there is no rule for this yet, and no event-by-event check against the original video to tell blinks, eye movements and tracking errors apart.
  • Area: the production area script still uses the three-point rule; the switch to the four-point rule is undecided. Blinks: the static per-frame analysis has a criterion and its check under the definition "not open = closed or partly covered" (step 10): one pixel threshold per mouse (Pluto 147.5, Terra 150.5 px, the method of Figure 10-1), 94% / 89% accurate on the blind frames, with the model's eyelid-distance error verified on 949 test frames at 2 px (Figures 10-5, 10-6); still missing are the definition of a blink itself and a rule that separates blinks from long half-covered stretches; Kaiwen's direction is "the eyelid height goes down and comes back up = one blink, a stricter version requires a moment of full closure".
