小鼠眼睛关键点追踪：做了什么，结果是什么Mouse eye keypoint tracking: what was done and what came out

Zhiheng Li (University of Minnesota), Kaiwen Sheng (Stanford) · 页面生成时间page built 2026-10-03 22:51 · GitHub: ZhihengLi0/mouse-behavior-tracking

任务：用 DeepLabCut 在小鼠眼睛视频的每一帧上找 8 个点：瞳孔上、下、左、右，上眼睑、下眼睑，鼻侧眼角、颞侧眼角。由这 8 个点得到瞳孔大小、眼睛睁开程度和眨眼，之后与脑记录对齐。

核心问题：拿到一个新视频，人要标多少帧，模型才够用？

工作分成两个阶段，区别在于瞳孔上点（pupil top）怎么标。两个阶段的误差数字不能直接比较。

第一阶段的结论（第 1–7 步）

前提：瞳孔上点标在看得见的、没有被眼睑挡住的瞳孔的最高处。

  1. batch size 用多少？用 2（第 2 步）。
  2. 用哪个模型？用 ResNet-50（第 3 步）。
  3. 同一个视频标多少帧够？约 80 帧就饱和，从 80 帧加到 300 帧，误差不变（第 4 步，只在第一只老鼠的 5 分钟视频上做过）。
  4. 用哪种算法挑要补标的帧？三种算法没有明显差距，jump 略微领先，之后都用 jump（第 4 步）。
  5. 模型的预测怎么用来找眨眼和出错的地方？把模型对每一帧的预测按时间画成曲线，不需要人工标注，就能认出三类事件：眨眼、眼球转动、模型把点跟丢（第 5 步）。
  6. 瞳孔面积怎么算？在这个标准下上点落在眼睑边上，所以只用左、右、下三个点（第 6 步）。

中间的逻辑：为什么换了标准还沿用 ResNet-50 和 batch 2

9 月 20 日起明确了瞳孔四个点的标法：把瞳孔看成椭圆，四个点是椭圆的四个端点。定义真正改变的只有瞳孔上点：从“看得见的瞳孔的最高处”改为“椭圆真实的最高点”。瞳孔下、左、右三个点原来就标在瞳孔边缘，这次只是明确为椭圆的端点；实际位置有小的调整（旧标注里左点比右点高约 32 px，新标法下左右两点在椭圆的同一条轴上）。上眼睑、下眼睑、鼻侧眼角、颞侧眼角的标法没有变。图像、任务和训练方式也都没有变。batch size 和模型的选择取决于图像和训练方式，不取决于瞳孔上点标在哪里，所以我们认为这两项选择不受影响，继续使用 ResNet-50 和 batch 2。这是一个判断，没有在新标准下重新比较过 batch size 和模型。

第二阶段的结论（第 8–12 步）

前提：把瞳孔看成椭圆，上点标在椭圆真实的最高点；被眼睑挡住时，由标注的人按椭圆估计它的位置。

  1. 同一个视频标多少帧够？和第一阶段的“约 80 帧饱和”一致：同一个 5 分钟视频在新标准下重新做，20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 帧的误差是 12.9 / 13.0 / 12.0 / 13.1 / 12.3 px，没有随帧数下降；之后每个视频的平台点也都不超过 80 帧（第 9 步）。
  2. 换一只新老鼠怎么办？要重新标，标 20 帧就到约 10 px。第二只老鼠（当时的模型只见过一只老鼠）：0 帧 208 px，标 20 帧到 10.4 px。第三只老鼠（模型已见过两只老鼠）：0 帧 26 px，标 20 帧到 9.2 px。第二次换老鼠时，不标任何帧的起点误差小得多（第 9 步）。
  3. 同一只老鼠之后的每个视频要标多少帧？标 0–40 帧就到平台，已完成的 9 个里有 1 个需要 80 帧（第 9 步）。
  4. 只标 5 帧或 10 帧够不够？多数视频不够，20 帧是在每个视频上都有效的最小批量（第 10 步）。
  5. 加入新视频会不会让旧视频变差？不会（第 11 步）。
  6. 瞳孔面积怎么算？新标准下四个点都是椭圆端点，用四个点算的面积误差约为三点法的一半（第 12 步）。

进度：已完成 11 个视频（视频 0–10）；视频 11 进行中，按 0 / 20 / 40 / … 帧的误差为 25.96 / 9.24 / 8.86 px。

Task: DeepLabCut finds 8 points on every frame of a mouse-eye video: pupil top, bottom, left, right; upper and lower eyelid; nasal and temporal eye corner. From these we get pupil size, eye opening and blinks, to be aligned with brain recordings later.

Main question: On a new video, how many frames must a person label before the model is good enough?

The work has two phases, which differ in how the pupil-top point is labeled. Error numbers of the two phases cannot be compared directly.

Conclusions of phase 1 (steps 1–7)

Premise: pupil top is placed at the highest point of the visible pupil, the part not hidden by the eyelid.

  1. Which batch size? 2 (step 2).
  2. Which model? ResNet-50 (step 3).
  3. How many labels are enough within one video? About 80 saturate it; from 80 to 300 labels the error does not change (step 4, done only on the 5-minute video of the first mouse).
  4. Which algorithm picks the frames to label next? The three algorithms show no clear gap; jump is slightly ahead and is the one used from then on (step 4).
  5. How do the predictions reveal blinks and failures? Plotting the model's prediction of every frame over time shows three kinds of events without any human labels: blinks, eye movements, and the model losing the points (step 5).
  6. How to compute the pupil area? Under this standard the top point sits on the lid, so only left, right and bottom are used (step 6).

The logic in between: why ResNet-50 and batch 2 were kept after the standard changed

From 2026-09-20 the way the four pupil points are labeled was made explicit: the pupil is treated as an ellipse and the four points are its four endpoints. Only the definition of pupil top really changed: from "the highest point of the visible pupil" to "the true top of the ellipse". Pupil bottom, left and right were already placed on the pupil edge and were only made explicit as ellipse endpoints; their positions were adjusted slightly (in the old labels the left point sat about 32 px higher than the right one; now both lie on the same axis of the ellipse). The labeling of the upper eyelid, lower eyelid, nasal corner and temporal corner did not change. The images, the task and the training procedure did not change either. The choice of batch size and model depends on the images and the training procedure, not on where the pupil-top point is placed, so we consider both choices unaffected and keep ResNet-50 with batch 2. This is a judgment; batch size and model were not compared again under the new standard.

Conclusions of phase 2 (steps 8–12)

Premise: the pupil is treated as an ellipse and pupil top is placed at the true top of that ellipse; where the eyelid hides it, the labeler estimates its position from the ellipse.

  1. How many labels are enough within one video? Consistent with "about 80 saturate" from phase 1: the same 5-minute video redone under the new standard gives 12.9 / 13.0 / 12.0 / 13.1 / 12.3 px at 20 / 40 / 60 / 80 / 100 labels, with no decrease; and no later video has a plateau point above 80 labels (step 9).
  2. What about a new mouse? It needs its own labels, and 20 of them reach about 10 px. Second mouse (the model had seen one mouse): 208 px at 0 labels, 10.4 px at 20. Third mouse (the model had seen two mice): 26 px at 0 labels, 9.2 px at 20. The second time the mouse changed, the starting error without any labels was far smaller (step 9).
  3. How many labels does each later video of the same mouse need? 0–40 reach the plateau; 1 of the 9 finished ones needed 80 (step 9).
  4. Are 5 or 10 labels enough? Not on most videos; 20 is the smallest batch that worked on every video (step 10).
  5. Do new videos make old ones worse? No (step 11).
  6. How to compute the pupil area? Under the new standard all four points are ellipse endpoints, and the 4-point area has about half the error of the 3-point rule (step 12).

Status: 11 videos finished (videos 0–10); video 11 in progress, error at 0 / 20 / 40 / … labels: 25.96 / 9.24 / 8.86 px.

怎么量误差（全页统一）：每个视频留出一组人工标注的测试帧，不参与训练，也不参与任何选择。对每一帧，算 8 个点的预测位置与人工标注之间的 RMSE（像素），再取所有测试帧的中位数。数字越小越好。作为参照：眼睛宽度约 320–390 px，所以 10 px 约为眼宽的 3%。

训练配置（第 2、3 步选定，两个阶段都使用）：ResNet-50，batch 2。每个模型都从 ImageNet 预训练权重开始重新训练，不接着我们自己之前的模型继续训。

How error is measured (same on the whole page): Each video has a set of human-labeled test frames that are never used for training or for any decision. For each frame we take the RMSE (pixels) between predicted and human positions over the 8 points, then the median over the test frames. Lower is better. For scale: the eye is about 320–390 px wide, so 10 px is about 3% of the eye width.

Training recipe (chosen in steps 2 and 3, used in both phases): ResNet-50, batch 2. Every model is trained anew from ImageNet-pretrained weights; it never continues from one of our earlier models.

1第一条缩放曲线（早期结果，已被后面的实验取代，仅作记录）First scaling curve (early result, superseded by the later experiments, kept for the record)

做了什么：2026 年 8 月。在 5 分钟视频的前 4 分钟里标 20、50、100 帧，分别训练 ResNet-50，都在最后 1 分钟的 100 帧测试帧上打分。

What was done:August 2026. 20, 50 and 100 frames from the first 4 minutes of the 5-minute video were labeled; a ResNet-50 was trained on each and scored on 100 test frames from the final minute.

结论：当时的结果是多标反而更差。原因查出来有两个：当时的标注前后不一致；内部验证集只有 5 帧，选不出好模型。于是在 9 月 10 日重新审核了全部标注，把验证集扩大到 20 帧。这一步的数字用的是旧标注，不能和后面的数字比较，也不再作为结论使用：“标多少帧够”的答案以第 4 步和第 9 步为准。它留在这里，只因为它是重新审核标注、扩大验证集的原因。

Conclusion:At the time, more labels made the result worse. Two causes were found: the labels of that time were inconsistent, and the internal validation set had only 5 frames, too few to pick a good model. All labels were re-reviewed on 2026-09-10 and the validation set was enlarged to 20 frames. The numbers of this step use the old labels, cannot be compared with later ones, and are no longer used as a conclusion: the answer to "how many labels are enough" comes from steps 4 and 9. The step is kept only because it is the reason the labels were re-reviewed and the validation set enlarged.

2选 batch size：batch 2Batch size: batch 2

做了什么：用审核后的标注（前 80 帧训练，后 20 帧验证），batch size 取 2、4、8、16 各训练一次（100 轮）。选择规则在看结果之前定好：内部验证损失最低的 batch。最后 1 分钟的测试帧只用来报告，不参与选择。

What was done:With the reviewed labels (first 80 frames train, last 20 validate), one run each at batch 2, 4, 8, 16 (100 epochs). The selection rule was fixed before looking at results: lowest internal validation loss. The final-minute test frames are report-only.

结论：选 batch 2。内部验证集和独立测试集选出同一个 batch，说明扩大后的验证集可以信任。每个 batch 只跑了一次，batch 之间的差距不大（21.1–23.6 px）。

Conclusion:Batch 2. The internal validation set and the independent test set pick the same batch, which shows the enlarged validation set can be trusted. One run per batch; the differences between batches are small (21.1–23.6 px).

3选模型：ResNet-50Backbone: ResNet-50

做了什么：同样的数据和设置（batch 2，100 轮），比较 5 种模型：ResNet-50、HRNet-W18 / W32 / W48、CSPNeXt-S。

What was done:Same data and settings (batch 2, 100 epochs), five backbones: ResNet-50, HRNet-W18 / W32 / W48, CSPNeXt-S.

结论：选 ResNet-50。验证损失分不出模型，按事先定好的规则用置信度决定；ResNet-50 同时误差最低、训练最快。此后所有实验都用 ResNet-50 + batch 2。

Conclusion:ResNet-50. Validation loss could not separate the models, so the pre-declared tie-break (confidence) decided; ResNet-50 also has the lowest error and trains fastest. All later experiments use ResNet-50 with batch 2.

4主动学习：让算法挑帧来补标，80 帧之后没有带来改善Active learning: algorithm-picked frames brought no gain beyond 80 labels

做了什么：从 80 帧起步，每轮让算法在视频里找“模型可能出错的帧”，人工标 20 帧加进去，重新训练。三种找帧算法各走一条线：置信度低的帧（uncertain）、关键点在相邻帧之间突然跳动的帧（jump）、偏离平滑轨迹的帧（fitting）。一共 11 轮，每条线从 80 帧加到 300 帧。

What was done:Starting from 80 labels, each round an algorithm searched the video for frames where the model is probably wrong; a person labeled 20 of them and the model was retrained. Three detectors ran as three branches: low confidence (uncertain), keypoints jumping between consecutive frames (jump), deviation from a smooth trajectory (fitting). 11 rounds, each branch from 80 to 300 labels.

结论：在这个视频上（第一阶段的标注标准），约 80 帧就已经饱和，加到 300 帧误差不变。三种算法没有明显差距，jump 略微领先：11 轮的平均中位误差是 jump 16.4 px、uncertain 16.8 px、fitting 17.0 px，11 轮里有 8 轮 jump 最低；但差距只有 0.4–0.6 px，小于同一设置重复训练时的波动，所以不能说 jump 确实更好。之后的实验统一用 jump 挑帧。新标准下在同一个视频上重做，结果一致（第 9 步，视频 0：20–100 帧都在 12–13 px）。更正记录：第 6 轮曾报告“误差下降”，后来查出是中途换了误差公式造成的，已撤回（9 月 18 日）；这张图是统一公式后重算的。从此规定：同一个指标只用同一个脚本、同一个公式。

Conclusion:On this video (phase-1 label standard) about 80 labels already saturate; 300 labels give the same error. The three detectors show no clear gap, with jump slightly ahead: the mean of the median error over the 11 rounds is 16.4 px for jump, 16.8 px for uncertain and 17.0 px for fitting, and jump is the lowest in 8 of the 11 rounds; but the gap is only 0.4–0.6 px, smaller than the run-to-run variation of one setting, so jump cannot be called truly better. All later experiments pick frames with jump. Redone on the same video under the new standard, the result is the same (step 9, video 0: 12–13 px anywhere from 20 to 100 labels). Correction on record: a "drop" reported at round 6 was traced to a change of error formula mid-series and was retracted (2026-09-18); this figure is recomputed with one formula. Rule since then: one metric, one script, one formula.

5时间序列：不用标注就能分出眨眼、眼动和追踪丢失Time series: blinks, eye movements and tracking loss without labels

做了什么：让模型预测前 4 分钟的每一帧（14,400 帧，60 帧/秒），把瞳孔中心位置、眼睛睁开高度、瞳孔面积、置信度随时间画出来。偏离 1 秒滚动中位数太远的帧标为异常（红点）。然后对每个大的异常，回到视频里逐个看是什么。

What was done:The model predicted every frame of the first 4 minutes (14,400 frames, 60 fps). Pupil-centre position, eye opening, pupil area and confidence were plotted over time; frames far from a 1-second rolling median are marked as anomalies (red dots). Each large anomaly was then checked in the video.

结论：三类事件可以这样分：眨眼 = 睁开高度和面积一起下降；眼动 = 中心位置移动而睁开高度不变；追踪丢失 = 置信度低、点突然跳远。置信度只作辅助信号，不能单独当作眨眼的证据。

Conclusion:The three kinds of events separate like this: blink = opening and area drop together; eye movement = centre moves while the opening stays; tracking loss = low confidence and points jumping far. Confidence is a secondary signal only and is not evidence of a blink by itself.

6瞳孔面积怎么算（旧标注标准）：用左、右、下三个点Pupil area (old label standard): from the left, right and bottom points

做了什么：当时的标注标准下，瞳孔上点标在“看得见的瞳孔”的最高处，瞳孔上缘常被上眼睑挡住，所以上点其实落在眼睑边上。比较了两种算法：四点椭圆（用上下左右四个点），和三点椭圆（左右两点定中心和宽度，下点定半高，不用上点）。另外实现了眨眼处理：眨眼期间的帧标为“不可信”，面积用前后插值代替。

What was done:Under the label standard of that time the pupil-top point was placed at the top of the visible pupil; the upper pupil edge is often hidden by the lid, so the top point really sat on the lid. Two rules were compared: a 4-point ellipse (all four points) and a 3-point ellipse (left and right give centre and width, bottom gives the half-height, top unused). Blink handling was added: frames during a blink are marked untrusted and the area is interpolated across them.

结论：在旧标注标准下，三点椭圆更准，于是面积脚本采用三点法加眨眼插值。这个结论在第 8 步换了标注标准之后有变化，见第 12 步。

Conclusion:Under the old label standard the 3-point ellipse was more accurate, so the area script used it together with blink interpolation. This changes after the label standard of step 8; see step 12.

7换一只新老鼠：旧模型不能用，标 20 帧就恢复A new mouse: the old model fails, 20 labels recover it

做了什么：拿第二只老鼠（Pluto）的一段 20 分钟视频。先把只在第一只老鼠上训练的模型直接用上去（0 帧），再每次加 20 帧 Pluto 的标注重新训练，直到 80 帧。测试集是 Pluto 视频里留出的 59 帧。这里第一次使用后来一直沿用的挑帧办法：第一批 20 帧按画面外观聚类挑选（不需要模型）；之后每批先让模型预测整段视频，找出关键点在相邻两帧之间跳动超过眼宽 3% 的帧，再从这些帧里聚类挑 20 帧。

What was done:A 20-minute video of a second mouse (Pluto). First the model trained only on the first mouse was applied unchanged (0 labels), then 20 Pluto labels were added at a time up to 80. The test set is 59 held-out Pluto frames. The frame-picking procedure used ever since was introduced here: the first 20 frames by clustering image appearance (no model needed); every later batch by predicting the whole video, flagging frames where a keypoint jumps by more than 3% of the eye width between consecutive frames, and clustering 20 frames out of those.

结论：换老鼠必须重新标，但 20 帧就能把误差从 185 px 降到约 14 px，80 帧到约 10 px。这是旧标注标准下的第一次尝试；新标准下更完整的比较（两次换老鼠）见第 9 步。

Conclusion:A new mouse needs its own labels, but 20 of them bring the error from 185 px to about 14 px, and 80 to about 10 px. This was the first attempt, under the old label standard; the fuller comparison under the new standard (two changes of mouse) is in step 9.

8新标注标准（9 月 20 日起）：瞳孔按椭圆标New label standard (from 2026-09-20): the pupil as an ellipse

做了什么：明确了瞳孔四个点的标法：把瞳孔看成一个椭圆，上、下、左、右四个点是椭圆的四个端点；被眼睑挡住的部分按椭圆补全，点标在推断出的位置上。定义改变的只有上点（原来标在看得见的瞳孔的最高处）；下、左、右三个点只是明确为椭圆端点；眼睑和眼角的四个点标法不变。9 月 26 日补充一条约定：瞳孔边缘出现两层轮廓时，标左边那层；黑色核心外面的一圈亮带算作瞳孔。之前的全部视频按新标准重新标注，从这一步起所有模型和数字都是新标准。

What was done:The labeling of the four pupil points was made explicit: the pupil is treated as an ellipse and top, bottom, left, right are its four endpoints; where the lid hides the pupil, the ellipse is completed and the point is placed at the inferred position. Only the definition of the top point changed (it used to be the highest point of the visible pupil); bottom, left and right were only made explicit as ellipse endpoints; the four eyelid and corner points are labeled as before. A convention added on 2026-09-26: where the pupil edge shows a double contour, the left one is labeled, and the bright band around the dark core counts as pupil. All earlier videos were relabeled; from here on every model and number uses the new standard.

结论：新标准下四个点都有明确的几何含义，面积可以直接由四个点算出。模型和 batch size 沿用第 2、3 步的选择（ResNet-50，batch 2）：改的只是点标在哪里，图像和训练方式没有变，所以没有重新比较。旧标准（第 1–7 步）的误差数字不能和新标准（第 9 步起）的数字直接比较。

Conclusion:Under the new standard all four points have a clear geometric meaning and the area follows directly from them. Model and batch size are carried over from steps 2 and 3 (ResNet-50, batch 2): only where the points are placed changed, not the images or the training procedure, so they were not compared again. Error numbers under the old standard (steps 1–7) cannot be compared directly with those under the new one (from step 9).

9主线：每个新视频要标多少帧Main line: labels needed per new video

做了什么：把视频一个一个加进来：视频 0 是第一只老鼠的 5 分钟视频，视频 1–10 是 Pluto 的 20 分钟视频，一天一个，从 2025-10-31 往前到 10-22（视频 1、2、3 是同一天）；视频 11 起是第三只老鼠 Terra，同样一天一个，从 2025-10-17 往前。每个视频的流程相同：

  1. 在视频最后 10% 里均匀取 50 帧作测试集，人工标好后冻结。
  2. 先用上一个模型直接预测这个视频，得到“0 帧”的误差。
  3. 每步加 20 帧这个视频的标注（挑帧办法见第 7 步；每批先由模型预标，人再逐帧改正），和之前视频带入的标注一起重新训练，在测试集上打分。
  4. 平台规则：连续两步都没有让最好成绩改善超过 3%，就停。平台点是最后一次有改善的那一步。
  5. 进入下一个视频，只带入这个视频到平台点为止的标注。这样做是为了模拟真实使用：到平台就不再标了。

What was done:Videos are added one at a time: video 0 is the 5-minute video of the first mouse, videos 1–10 are 20-minute videos of Pluto, one recording day each, going back from 2025-10-31 to 10-22 (videos 1, 2, 3 are the same day); from video 11 on the videos are of a third mouse, Terra, again one day each, going back from 2025-10-17. Every video follows the same steps:

  1. 50 frames evenly spaced over the final 10% of the video become the test set; they are labeled and frozen.
  2. The previous model is applied unchanged to get the "0 labels" error.
  3. Each step adds 20 labels of this video (picked as in step 7; the model pre-labels each batch and a person corrects every frame), retrains together with the labels carried from earlier videos, and scores on the test set.
  4. Plateau rule: stop when two consecutive steps each fail to improve the best error by more than 3%. The plateau point is the last step that did improve it.
  5. The next video starts, carrying only this video's labels up to the plateau point. This mirrors real use, where labeling stops at the plateau.

The first video of a new mouse needs 20 labels; later videos of the same mouse mostly reach the plateau with 20, at least 0 and at most 80. The error at the plateau is mostly between 3 and 16 px (video 8 excepted).

11加入新视频会不会让旧视频变差：不会Do new videos hurt old ones: no

做了什么：把这条线上训练过的每一个模型，拿到每一个视频的测试集上打分（回测）。

What was done:Every model trained along the sequence was scored on the test set of every video (back-test).

结论：一个模型可以一直累加视频，旧视频不会变差。同一天的视频可以互相直接用（视频 3 在没有自己标注时就已经 4.4 px）；换一天则要重新看 0 帧误差再决定标多少。只看单个模型的 0 帧成绩不可靠，个别模型会突然差很多。

Conclusion:One model can keep accumulating videos without hurting the old ones. Videos of the same day transfer directly (video 3 was at 4.4 px before any of its own labels); a new day needs its 0-label error checked before deciding how much to label. A 0-label score from a single model is not reliable; individual models can be far off.

12瞳孔面积和眨眼（新标注标准）Pupil area and blinks (new label standard)

做了什么：换成椭圆标注标准后，重新比较面积的算法：三点法（第 6 步的做法）、四点法（宽 × 上下高度），以及“每帧去掉一个点”的几种变体。以人工标注的四点椭圆面积为真值。同时在多个视频上检查现有眨眼规则：哪个信号最能把人工认定的闭眼帧分出来。

What was done:With the ellipse label standard the area rules were compared again: the 3-point rule (step 6), the 4-point rule (width × top-to-bottom height) and several "drop one point" variants. Truth = the 4-point ellipse area from the human labels. The current blink rule was also checked on several videos: which signal best separates the frames a person marked as eye-closed.

结论：新标准下四点法的面积误差约为三点法的一半（3.7% 对 6.9%），建议改用四点法；正式的面积脚本目前仍是三点法，等确认后再换。现行眨眼规则是在视频 0 上调的，在新老鼠上标得过多，需要重新设计。B 图只有 16 个人工认定的闭眼帧，样本很少。

Conclusion:Under the new standard the 4-point area error is about half that of the 3-point rule (3.7% vs 6.9%), so switching is recommended; the production area script still uses the 3-point rule until that is approved. The current blink rule was tuned on video 0, flags far too much on the new mouse, and needs to be redesigned. Panel B rests on only 16 human-marked closed-eye frames.

13还没有结论的地方Where there is no conclusion yet

  • 平台规则在噪声边缘：测试集只有 50 帧，同一设置重复训练，中位误差本身会有约 0.5–1 px 的波动，而 3% 约等于 0.3 px。所以每个视频的平台点有大约 ±20 帧的不确定性。每个点只训练了一次。
  • 预标带来的偏向：测试集是人在模型预标的基础上改出来的，做预标的那个模型在这组测试集上会显得比实际好（视频 5、6 最明显）。
  • 跳动规则找错帧找得准不准还没有量过：被标出的帧里有多少真的错，没被标出的帧里漏了多少。
  • 视频 8 的误差一直在 25 px 以上，原因未明。视频 4 画面质量差。
  • 第三只老鼠（Terra，视频 11–18）刚开始做第一个视频（数字见第 9 步的表）；在它做完之前，“新老鼠需要 20 帧”只在一只新老鼠上验证过。
  • 面积和眨眼：是否换成四点法、眨眼规则怎么改，待定。
  • The plateau rule works at the noise floor: With 50 test frames, retraining the same setting moves the median by about 0.5–1 px, while 3% is about 0.3 px. Each video's plateau point therefore carries an uncertainty of roughly ±20 labels. Every point is a single training run.
  • Anchoring by pre-labels: Test sets are made by correcting a model's pre-labels, so the model that made them looks better than it is on that test set (clearest on videos 5 and 6).
  • How well the jump rule finds wrong frames has not been measured: how many flagged frames are really wrong, and how many wrong frames it misses.
  • Video 8 stays above 25 px for an unknown reason. Video 4 has poor image quality.
  • The third mouse (Terra, videos 11–18) has just started with its first video (numbers in the table of step 9); until it is finished, "a new mouse needs 20 labels" rests on one new mouse.
  • Area and blinks: the switch to the 4-point rule and the redesign of the blink rule are undecided.
