# Blind pre-labels, video 1 supplement (img101-img110): low-confidence images

Placed by looking at the 10 PNG frames only (brightened, with a coordinate grid; no model output used).
Same standard as the first blind set: pupil = four end points of the pupil ellipse on the outer edge of the
dark disc, extrapolated under the lids; eyelid_top/bottom = lid margins on the vertical through the pupil
centre; corners = nasal (left) and temporal (right) tips of the eye opening.

- Points placed: 80 (10 frames with all 8 points).
- Images with no point: none.

Low-confidence images (all ten are squint or half-open frames; the pupil is a wide dark band and its top and
bottom are hidden under the lids, so all pupil_top / pupil_bottom points are extrapolated):
- img101, img103, img104, img105, img106, img109, img110: eye narrowed to about 120-130 px; pupil edges diffuse,
  width taken from the darkest core (about 270 px); corners uncertain (the nasal shadow runs far to the left)
- img102, img107: eye half open; pupil_top extrapolated about 40 px above the lid
- img108: eye open, large pupil (about 270 px wide); pupil_top at the lid margin, uncertain
